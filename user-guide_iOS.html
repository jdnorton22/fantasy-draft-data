1. Overview

FFL Draft Day Picker Pro is a fully-featured manual draft tracker for American Fantasy Football. Use it live during your draft to record picks, get AI recommendations, track positional scarcity, and analyze every team's roster.

Key features:

  • AI-powered pick recommendations with VBD, scarcity, and production analysis
  • Mock drafts against AI opponents with realistic variance
  • Player Search with AI Draft Advice for pre-draft research
  • Trade Analyzer comparing 8 dimensions with Pros/Cons verdicts
  • Saved Drafts with Team Report Cards and curved grading
  • Two color schemes (Standard Live / Blue-Grey)
  • QB Flex Mode for superflex leagues
  • Full Draft Board grid with serpentine/linear support

2. Draft Setup

Configure your league before starting the draft.

League Settings

  • League Name — identifies your draft in exports and saved boards
  • Teams — set count (2–20) and name each team
  • Rounds — how many rounds to draft (default: 16)
  • Draft Type — Snake (order reverses each round) or Linear (same order every round)
  • Linear Rounds First — for keeper leagues: draft linearly for N rounds, then switch to snake

Position Requirements

Set min/max roster slots per position. The AI uses these to determine when minimums are filled and what to recommend next.

QB Flex Mode

Toggle on for superflex/2QB premium leagues. The AI will prioritize QBs 16 picks ahead in recommendations, reflecting their premium value in these formats.

Other Settings

  • Player Sort Order — ESPN Rank, ADP, or VBD
  • Draft Grading — Curved (relative to other teams) or Raw
  • Stopwatch — tracks pick time (count-up, no auto-draft)
  • My Team — select your team for personalized recommendations
  • Mock Draft Mode — AI opponents auto-pick when enabled

3. Main Draft Screen

Tabbed Info Card (Pick · Summary · Draft)

The card at the top of the screen has three tabs:

  • Pick — who's on the clock, current pick/round, progress bar
  • Summary — position counts with a 3-way toggle: My Team | On Clock | League. Tap the team icon to open the Draft Board grid.
  • Draft — Mock draft controls, My Team selector (changeable mid-draft), and Color Scheme toggle (Standard Live / Blue-Grey)

Making a Pick

  1. The Pick tab shows who's on the clock
  2. Use the Best Available card or tap View Board
  3. Confirm the pick when prompted
  4. The draft advances automatically (snake order reverses each round)
Tip: Tap Undo to reverse the last pick if there's a mistake.

4. AI Draft Advisor

The AI scores every available player using a weighted composite of multiple factors.

Scoring Weights

FactorWeightWhat It Measures
Value35%ADP vs current pick number
Scarcity22%Remaining startable players at position
Team Need22%Roster gaps vs position requirements
Bye Week11%Position-aware schedule conflict detection
Elite Tier10%Top positional talent bonus (top 3/5/10)

Bonuses

  • 🔥 TD Production: RB/WR/TE with 6–9 TDs = +3, 10–14 = +5, 15+ = +8
  • 🏃 Yardage: RB/WR/TE with 1000–1499 yards = +3, 1500+ = +5
  • ⚡ QB Flex: When enabled, QBs get +8 flat bonus and value calculated 16 picks ahead

Penalties

  • Injury: OUT/IR/DOUBTFUL players excluded from recommendations entirely
  • Questionable: Excluded from all Best Available tabs
  • Reach: 9-15 picks early = -5, 16-25 = -12, 26-40 = -20, 40+ = -30

Hard Rules

  • Fill minimums first: Until every position minimum is met (QB, RB, WR, TE, K, DST), only positions that still need filling are recommended
  • Position max: Never recommends exceeding a position cap
  • K/DST suppression: Excluded from depth picks until 15% of each have been drafted league-wide
Tip: Look for 🔥 TD producer and 🏃 Yardage producer tags in AI reasons — these players have proven production floors.

5. Mock Draft Mode

Practice your strategy against AI opponents.

How It Works

  1. Select your team in the Draft tab
  2. Toggle Mock on
  3. AI opponents auto-pick; when it's your turn, the draft pauses
  4. Make your pick manually, then AI resumes

6. Player Search

Browse all players from the menu — including those already drafted.

Features

  • Search by name, position, or NFL team
  • Position filter tabs (All, QB, RB, WR, TE, K, DST)
  • Sort by ESPN Rank or ADP (segmented toggle)
  • Player count indicator
  • Drafted players shown at 50% opacity

AI Draft Advice

Tap any player to see their detail card with AI Draft Advice — shown automatically when viewing from Player Search:

  • Position Tier: Elite / High-End / Mid-Range / Low-End / Bench — with thresholds tailored to your league size and position requirements
  • Draft Window: Round estimate based on ADP ÷ team count
  • 🔥 TD Production: Analysis of last-year touchdowns with reliability commentary
  • 🏃 Yardage: Total yards analysis with floor/ceiling guidance
  • Value Over Replacement: Dynamic baseline from league config (min starters × teams)
  • Injury Risk: Discount guidance for injured/questionable players

View On Board

Undrafted players (when draft is active) show a blue "View On Board" button. Tap to jump directly to the draft board with that player's name pre-searched — ready to draft.

7. Trade Analyzer

Compare trade scenarios across 8 dimensions with AI-generated Pros and Cons.

How to Use

  1. Open Trade Analyzer from the menu
  2. Add players to "Giving Up" (your side) and "Receiving"
  3. The comparison chart updates automatically
  4. Review the verdict (Good / Even / Bad) with detailed Pros and Cons

Comparison Dimensions

Verdict Grading

  • Good Trade (green) — you win 6-8 of 8 categories
  • Even Trade (yellow) — you win 3-5 categories
  • Bad Trade (red) — you win 0-2 categories

Saving Trades

Tap Save to store a trade scenario. Names are auto-generated (e.g., "B. Robinson, P. Nacua → J. Gibbs"). Open saved trades with the folder icon. Duplicates are automatically replaced.

8. Saved Drafts & Report Cards

Saving a Draft

When a draft completes, confetti drops and you're prompted to save. Options:

  • Save & Export CSV — saves the board and opens the share sheet
  • Save Draft — saves for future reference
  • View Draft Grades — opens analytics immediately

Viewing Saved Drafts

Access from menu → Saved Drafts. Each entry shows league name, team/round count, and save date. Tap to open the full Draft Board grid (same interactive board as during the live draft). Swipe to delete individual entries or use "Delete All".

Team Report Cards

From a saved draft's board view, tap the report icon (🔍) to see ranked teams. Each report card includes:

  • Curved Grade (A+ to F) — based on composite score relative to all teams
  • Stats Grid — avg value over ADP, total TDs, total yards, elite picks count
  • Highlights — best pick (biggest steal) and worst pick (biggest reach)
  • Roster Breakdown — position counts with min-met indicators
  • VS League Average — ADP, VBD, TDs, yards compared with green/red color coding
  • Bye Week Calendar — week-by-week player list with conflict warnings
  • Health Risks — injured/questionable players drafted

10. Pro Tips

Draft Day Strategy

  • Use the 🤖 AI tab as your primary guide — it balances value, need, and scarcity automatically
  • The AI fills all starting minimums before recommending depth — trust the process
  • Watch for 🔥 TD and 🏃 Yardage tags — proven production beats projections
  • Check the Summary tab (My Team) to track your roster balance in real-time
  • Tag sleepers as ⭐ favorites before the draft for quick access via the star filter

Mock Draft Practice

  • Run multiple mocks from different draft positions to find your scarcity windows
  • AI opponents use weighted randomization — each mock plays out differently
  • Opponents don't enforce minimums-first, so you'll see K/DST taken early sometimes

Understanding VBD

  • VBD = baseline overall rank − player overall rank
  • Baseline = the Nth player at a position (N = min starters × team count)
  • Higher VBD = bigger advantage over replacement-level
  • VBD recalculates when you change position requirements or team count

Trade Analyzer Tips

  • Add players to both sides and review all 8 dimensions — not just one metric
  • The Pros/Cons section calls out elite talent being gained or lost by name
  • Save scenarios to compare different trade offers side by side
