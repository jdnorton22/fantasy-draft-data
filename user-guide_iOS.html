1Getting started

FFL Draft Day Picker helps you run and track a fantasy football draft. Use it live during your league draft to record picks and get recommendations, or run practice mock drafts against AI opponents.

The app opens with a short animated splash. On a fresh install it then lands on Draft Setup. Once a draft is started, the app opens straight to it each time until you reset.

Player rankings, ADP, injury status and headshots come from ESPN data that is refreshed nightly on our servers. Refresh before your draft, then work offline during it.

First launch: the app notes that your player data has never been refreshed. Tap OK and it fetches the latest rankings for you right away. Nothing is reset, and if the fetch fails the bundled data keeps working.
Appearance: the app is dark by default (the "Ice" theme) and has a light mode too. Switch it from the ··· menu or in Draft Settings. Your draft is saved automatically as you go.

2Setting up a draft

Setup is four short steps with a progress bar at the top. Use Next and Back to move through them, then Start Draft on the last one.

Step 1League
Name, number of teams, rounds, scoring format.
Step 2Rules
Draft type, stopwatch, position requirements, sort and grading, recommendations.
Step 3Teams & data
Name the teams, mark yours, mock draft, player data.
Step 4Draft order
Review the order, trade picks, and confirm.

League

  • League name - shown on exports and saved drafts.
  • Teams - 2 to 20, with a minus/plus stepper.
  • Rounds - how many rounds to draft.
  • Scoring format - Standard Half PPR Full PPR

Rules

  • Draft type - Snake (order reverses each round) or Linear (same order every round).
  • Linear rounds first - for keeper leagues. Draft linearly for a set number of rounds, then switch to snake.
  • Show stopwatch - tracks how long each team takes to pick.
  • Position requirements - the roster slots the app uses to judge needs and what to suggest next.
  • Sort & grading - sort players by ESPN Rank, ADP or VBD, and grade drafts Curved (relative to other teams) or Raw Score (absolute pick value).
  • Recommendations - QB Flex Mode (superflex and 2QB leagues), Hide Injured/Questionable, and Defer K/DST to the last 3 rounds.

Teams & data

  • Team names - tap a name to edit it. Randomize Draft Order shuffles the slots.
  • Your team - tap the flag beside a team so the app can highlight your picks and tailor advice.
  • Mock Draft Mode - turns on once your team is marked. See Mock drafts.
  • Player data - refresh rankings from the server.

Draft order

The last step shows the full pick order and lets you trade picks before the draft begins. Then tap the glowing Confirm the draft order card. It pulses ice-blue until you confirm, then turns green. Start Draft stays off until it is confirmed.

Note: Most league settings lock once the first pick is made, so set them before you start. Changing the number of teams, rounds or draft type in setup clears any pick trades you entered, because the pick grid changed.

3Finding your way around

The whole app lives under one header. There is no bottom tab bar.

  • Header - the app logo and name, the gear (Draft Settings) and the ··· menu.
  • Tab strip - scrolls sideways. The tabs come first, then the tool pills.
  • Context bar - always shows the round and pick, who is on the clock, a progress bar, and the stopwatch if it is on.

  • The beside Draft shows a draft is in progress (from the first pick until the draft is complete or reset).
  • The badges count the players on the Players tab and the teams on the Teams tab.
  • Every sheet closes with the same Done pill in the top right. Sheets that would throw away what you typed also keep a Cancel.

4The Draft tab

The Draft tab is a scrolling stack of cards, top to bottom.

Overall pick

The big number is the overall pick, with the round and your league name. A Your pick pill appears when you are on the clock. If you marked your team, the Mock On / Mock Off pill turns mock draft on or off right here.

Pick strip

A row of upcoming picks, starting with the current one (outlined in blue), so you can see who drafts next. Picks that were traded are marked.

Best Available

The recommendation card. Its filter pills pick what it shows, and the card is the same one you see everywhere else. See The player card and Best Available and the AI advisor.

Needs

Position counts against your requirements. Flip between On Clock (the team picking now) and My Team.

Picks

A list of every pick made, oldest first with the newest at the bottom, about five rows tall. It scrolls to the latest pick on its own. Each row shows a team logo, the round and pick, the player, and a health tag. Tap a row to open that player.

Controls

  • Undo - reverses the last pick.
  • Reset Draft - clears all picks and keeps your teams and settings.

Making a pick

  1. Check the Overall pick card, or the context bar, to confirm who is on the clock.
  2. Tap Draft This Player on Best Available, or open any player from the Players tab, Board, History or a tier list.
  3. Confirm the pick when asked.
  4. The draft advances automatically, and the next team goes on the clock.
Mistake? Tap Undo to reverse the last pick. Undo only works on picks. Pick trades have their own undo.

5The player card

One card is used everywhere a player appears: Best Available, the Players tab, the Board, History, and tier lists. Tapping a player anywhere opens it in a sheet. On Best Available, the person button at the right of the buttons opens it as well.

  • Header - headshot, name, position, team logo and bye week, ESPN rank and position rank, and last season's stat line.
  • Four stat tiles - ADP, VBD (value over a replacement-level starter), Tier and Proj (projected points).
  • ESPN and Depth - open the player's ESPN profile or the team's depth chart.
  • Stat breakdown - the AI's reasons as bars (Value, Scarcity, Team Need, Bye Week, Elite Tier, and more). Tap its header to fold it away. The card remembers your choice, and folds it by itself when the draft starts so the buttons stay in view.
  • Draft This Player - drafts him for the team on the clock. A thin line sits above it and the Player Info button.
  • AI top 5 - five tappable players below the buttons. See below.

Drafted players

A player who is already drafted can still be looked at. The draft button is greyed and reads Drafted by <team> · <round>.<pick>, for example "Drafted by Pocket Aces · 1.06".

AI top 5

When you open a player from the Players tab, History, or a tier list, the card also shows the AI's five best picks right now. Tap one to switch the whole card to that player, with his own Stat breakdown. The one on screen is outlined in blue. If he is not yet drafted, Draft This Player works for him too.

Best Available itself does not repeat the AI top 5, since the card is already showing the AI's first choice.

6Best Available and the AI advisor

The filter pills across the top of the Best Available card change what it shows:

  • AI - the recommendation engine's top suggestion for your roster, with the reasons behind it.
  • Overall - the highest-ranked available player.
  • Favs - the next available player you tagged as a favorite.
  • QB RB WR TE K DST - the best available at that position.

How the AI picks

The engine scores every available player on weighted factors, then adds production bonuses and injury penalties.

FactorWhat it measures
ValueThe player's ADP compared with the current pick number.
ScarcityHow many startable players remain at the position.
Team NeedGaps in your roster against your position requirements.
Bye WeekBye-week conflicts with players you already drafted.
Elite TierA bonus for top-tier talent at a position.

The engine also rewards proven touchdown and yardage production and steers away from injured players. It fills your starting requirements before suggesting depth, never goes past a position maximum, and holds off on kickers and defenses until late (see Defer K/DST).

Tiers

Players are grouped into tiers by VBD. A T3 chip shows a player's tier. On the card, the Tier tile warns when he is the last in his tier, meaning the next player at that position is a real step down. Tap the tier to open the whole tier list, and draft from there.

7The Players tab

Every player in the pool, with search and filters.

  • Search - by player name.
  • Filter chips - Healthy comes first and is off by default (turn it on to hide injured players). Drafted shows players already taken. The row scrolls sideways, with the position filters after them.
  • Sort - ESPN Rank, name, position or ADP.

Each player is a two-line card:

Team logo · NYJ · WR4 · Bye 9 · ADP 14 · VBD +32
  • Line 1 - name, a health chip (Q questionable, D doubtful, OUT), the tier chip, and rank.
  • Line 2 - team logo and abbreviation, position rank, bye week, ADP and VBD.
  • The star and bomb badge marks favorites and busts. Tap it to cycle none, favorite, bust.
  • Tap a card to open the player card.

Position colors are the same everywhere in the app, so a quick glance tells you QB, RB, WR, TE, K or DST.

8The Board

A full grid of the draft: one column for each team, one row for each round. Scroll in any direction. Each cell shows the player drafted there, colored by position. Tap a cell to open that player's card.

If picks were traded, the cell stays in its original slot, and a yellow pill across the middle names the team that really has the pick (or took the player).

9Teams and History

Teams

Every roster at a glance, with position counts against your requirements. Use it to see where a rival is thin before you pick.

History

Every pick made, in order, as the same player cards you see on the Players tab. Each shows its round and pick, such as 2.04. Two menus at the top filter the list by Team and Position, with a count of the picks that match. Tap a card to open the player. His AI top 5 is there too, so you can switch to an undrafted player and draft him directly.

The footer has Analytics and Undo, and Share exports the list.

10Trading picks

If teams agreed to trade picks in your league, the app can follow the trades so the right team is always on the clock. Trades can be entered during setup or at any time mid-draft, for picks that have not been made yet.

  1. Open Swap Picks in the tab strip (during the draft), or use Draft order in setup.
  2. Choose two teams. Your own team is labeled "(You)".
  3. Tap the picks each team gives up. Either side can give one pick or several, and the counts can differ.
  4. Tap Swap picks. Repeat for every trade.
  • Each pick shows as 1.03 (round, then pick in the round) with its overall number. Picks already made are locked and marked "Made".
  • A traded pick shows a swap icon and "via <original team>".
  • Undo last swap removes the most recent trade. It is separate from Undo for picks.
  • Trades take effect at once: the team on the clock updates, and a running mock draft re-checks whether it is your turn.
  • The Board and the pick strip show traded picks.

11Mock drafts

Practice your strategy against AI opponents.

  1. Mark your team in setup, or on the Draft Settings screen.
  2. Turn on Mock Draft Mode, or tap the Mock On pill on the Overall pick card.
  3. The other teams draft automatically. When it is your turn, the draft pauses.
  4. Make your pick, and the AI opponents carry on.

AI difficulty

  • Normal - opponents reach often, so more value falls to you.
  • Harder - realistic opponents that mostly draft by the book, with occasional reaches.
  • Tough - opponents rarely miss and almost always take the best available.
Tip: opponents use weighted randomness, so every mock plays out differently. Run several from different draft slots to learn where value tends to fall.

12Trade Analyzer and Favorites & Busts

Trade Analyzer

For player trades in a keeper or season-long league. Add players to Giving Up and Receiving. The comparison updates across eight factors: VBD total, average ADP, average rank, position value, total touchdowns, total yards, health risk and bye-week conflicts. A verdict rates the trade as good, even or bad, and you can save scenarios to compare offers.

Favorites & Busts

Tag players as favorites or busts to personalize your board. Tagging cycles none, favorite, bust. Favorites feed the Favs filter on Best Available. Tags carry over between sessions and data refreshes.

Saved drafts and report cards

When a draft finishes, you are asked to save it. From a saved draft's board you can open Team Report Cards: a letter grade for each team (Curved or Raw Score, per your setting) with roster breakdowns, best and worst picks, and comparisons with the league average.

14Draft settings

Tap the gear at the top right of the header. The settings use the same cards as setup:

  • Before the first pick - everything from setup is editable: league name, rounds, draft type, stopwatch, scoring, position requirements, sort and grading, recommendations, teams, mock draft and player data.
  • After the first pick - a shorter set stays open: draft progress, Pick swaps (opens the swap sheet), teams, mock draft, scoring, sort and grading, recommendations, position requirements and appearance.

You can change which team is yours, or turn mock draft on or off, at any point. Tap Done to close.

Note: Your draft saves automatically, so you can leave the app and come back to the same board.
