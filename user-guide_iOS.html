1. Getting Started

FFL Draft Day Picker Pro is a fully-featured manual draft tracker for American Fantasy Football. It uses free ESPN public data baked into the app — no account or API key required.

The app is designed to be used live during your draft. As each team makes their pick, you record it in the app. In return, you get real-time analytics, AI-powered recommendations, and full visibility into positional scarcity.

📋 Track Every Pick

Record picks as they happen. Full draft history with undo support.

🤖 AI Recommendations

Get smart pick suggestions based on value, need, scarcity, and bye weeks.

📊 Live Analytics

Draft grades, positional breakdowns, and value tracking updated in real time.

🎯 Mock Draft Mode

Practice against AI opponents that draft with realistic strategy.

2. Draft Setup

When you first open the app, you'll see the Draft Setup screen. Configure your league settings here before starting.

League Settings

  • League Name — Name your league (shows in exports and reports)
  • Teams — Set the number of teams (2–20) and name each one
  • Rounds — How many rounds to draft (default: 16)
  • Draft Type — Serpentine (snake) or Linear
  • Linear Rounds First — For keeper leagues: draft linearly for N rounds, then switch to serpentine

Stopwatch

When enabled, a stopwatch tracks how long each team takes to make their pick. This is a count-up timer for awareness — it won't auto-draft.

3. Drafting Players

Once your draft is configured and started, you'll see the main draft screen with the Best Available card, controls, and pick history.

Making a Pick

  1. The current team on the clock is displayed at the top
  2. Use the Best Available card to draft the recommended player directly, or
  3. Tap "View Board" to open the full player list, search, filter, and select any available player
  4. Confirm the pick when prompted

Undo

Made a mistake? Tap the undo button in the draft history to reverse the last pick. The player returns to the available pool and the draft backs up one slot.

Reset

Need to start over? The Reset button clears all picks and returns to round 1. Your league configuration (teams, settings) is preserved.

Draft Flow

Serpentine (Snake): Order reverses each round. Round 1: picks 1→10, Round 2: picks 10→1, Round 3: 1→10, etc.

Linear: Same order every round. Pick 1 always drafts first.

Hybrid (Keeper): Draft linearly for a set number of rounds (keeper rounds), then switch to serpentine for the remaining rounds.

4. AI Draft Advisor

The AI advisor scores every available player and recommends the optimal pick based on multiple factors.

Scoring Components

FactorWeightWhat It Measures
Value30%How much value you're getting vs the player's ADP
Positional Scarcity25%How many startable players remain at this position
Team Need25%Whether your roster needs this position based on requirements
Bye Week10%Scheduling conflicts with players already on your roster
Elite Tier10%Bonus for top-tier positional talent (top 5 at position)

Bonuses

  • TD Production: RB/WR/TE with 6+ TDs last season get +3 to +8 points
  • Yardage Production: RB/WR/TE with 1,000+ total yards get +3, 1,500+ get +5

Penalties

  • Injury: Players marked OUT/IR/DOUBTFUL receive a -40 point penalty
  • K/DST Early: Kickers and defenses are suppressed until core positions are filled (up to -30)
  • Bye Conflict: Position-aware — penalizes heavily when same-position players share a bye week (QB conflicts are penalized most since you only carry 2)

Hard Rules

  • The AI will never recommend a player at a position that has hit its configured maximum
  • K and DST are excluded from recommendations until the draft is 75% complete

Bye Week Intelligence

The advisor actively avoids bye week collisions. When a player has a clear bye week (no conflicts with your roster), you'll see:

✅ Bye 6 clear — no roster conflicts

When there's a conflict, especially at the same position:

⚠️ Bye 11 — 1 QB already on same bye

6. Player Details

Tap the player card icon on the Best Available card, or tap any player in the board view, to see their full detail sheet.

What You'll See

  • Full player header with headshot, position, team, bye week
  • ESPN profile link (opens in browser)
  • Last year's stats breakdown
  • Rankings: ESPN rank, ADP, position rank, custom rank (if set)
  • Pick Analysis — contextual insights about value, reach, and need

AI Analysis Breakdown

The AI Analysis section shows the player's total score and a detailed breakdown of each scoring component with individual progress bars:

  • 📈 Value — points from ADP vs pick position
  • 👥 Scarcity — points from positional supply
  • ✅ Team Need — points from roster gaps
  • 📅 Bye Week — points from schedule clearance
  • 👑 Elite Tier — bonus for top talent
  • 🔥 TD Bonus / 🏃 Yardage Bonus — production bonuses
  • ❌ Injury / ⚠️ K/DST Early — penalty deductions

Top AI Picks Comparison

Below the analysis, you'll see the AI's top 5 recommended picks ranked by score. Tap any player to view their detail card for comparison.

7. Mock Draft Mode

Practice your draft strategy against AI opponents. Enable Mock Draft Mode in setup after selecting your team.

How It Works

  1. Select your team in the "My Team" section
  2. Toggle "Mock Draft Mode" on
  3. Start the draft
  4. Other teams auto-draft using the AI advisor with weighted randomization
  5. When it's your turn, the draft pauses and waits for your pick

AI Behavior

AI teams don't always pick the #1 recommendation. They use weighted randomization across the top 5 picks:

  • #1 pick: 40% chance
  • #2 pick: 25% chance
  • #3 pick: 18% chance
  • #4 pick: 10% chance
  • #5 pick: 7% chance

This creates realistic draft variance — you won't see the same mock draft twice.

Tip: You can toggle mock draft on/off mid-draft from the settings menu. Turn it off to manually control all teams, or back on to let the AI resume.

Custom Rankings

You can set a custom rank on any player to move them up or down your personal board. Custom-ranked players show a pencil icon and sort by their custom position.

10. Player Data & Refresh

Player data comes from ESPN public rankings bundled with the app. You can refresh it from a remote source for the latest updates.

Data Sources (Priority Order)

  1. Custom import file — If you've imported a custom JSON file, it takes priority
  2. Remote cache — Data fetched from the server, cached for 24 hours
  3. Bundle — The players.json shipped with the app

Refreshing Data

Tap "Refresh Player Data" from the draft settings menu. This downloads the latest rankings from the server and resets the current draft. Your league configuration and user preferences (favorites, custom ranks) are preserved.

Automatic Updates

When the app is updated with new bundled player data, the cache is automatically invalidated and the draft resets on next launch. No manual uninstall needed.

Note: Refreshing player data will reset all draft picks. Your team setup and preferences are kept.

11. Pro Tips

Draft Day Strategy

  • Use the 🤖 AI tab as your primary guide — it balances value, need, and scarcity automatically
  • Check the bye week indicator before drafting. Stacking too many players on the same bye leaves you thin for that week
  • Tag your sleepers as favorites before the draft starts so you can quickly find them with the ⭐ filter
  • Don't reach for K/DST early — the AI suppresses them until round 12+ for good reason

Mock Draft Practice

  • Run multiple mock drafts from different draft positions to practice various strategies
  • The AI uses weighted randomization, so each mock plays out differently
  • Pay attention to which positions dry up at your draft slot — this reveals your scarcity windows

Understanding VBD

  • VBD (Value Based Drafting) measures how much better a player is than the last starter at their position
  • High VBD = the player provides a bigger advantage over the replacement-level option
  • VBD naturally favors RB/WR early (bigger gaps between starters and replacements) and deprioritizes K/DST (minimal difference between options)
