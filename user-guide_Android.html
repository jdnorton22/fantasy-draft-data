Getting started

FFL Draft Day Picker recommends a pick for your team as each selection comes off the board, grades the finished draft, and lets you practice with mock drafts against the AI. After your player data is downloaded, the app works without a connection.

  1. Refresh your player data. On first launch a welcome message offers Refresh Now or Maybe Later. The data bundled with the app can be out of date, so refresh once before your draft.
  2. Complete the 4-step setup. The first time you open the app it takes you to Setup. See Setup.
  3. Draft. Use the Draft tab to follow recommendations and record picks.
  4. Save and review. When the last pick is made, the Draft Grades screen opens. Tap Save Draft there to keep the draft.

Across the top are the tabs Draft, Board, Players, Teams and History, plus the shortcuts Trade Analyzer and Favorites & Busts. The gear icon opens settings and the three-dot icon opens more options. A bar under the tabs shows the pick on the clock and a progress line.

Setup (4 steps)

Setup runs through four steps. Tap a step at the top, or use Back and Next. The last step has Save and Draft. You can reopen setup any time with the gear icon.

Step 1: League

  • League Name, shown on the Draft screen.
  • Number of Rounds, from 6 to 30.
  • Scoring Format: Standard, Half PPR or Full PPR.
  • Player Sort Order: ESPN Expert Rank or ADP (Average Draft Position). This sets how Best Available ranks players.
  • Draft Grade Mode: Curve (Relative to League) or Scale (Absolute Thresholds).
  • What is VBD drafting? A link that explains value based drafting.
  • Color scheme: position colors in Standard, ESPN, FFL or Sleeper styles. Tap the info icon for a legend.

Step 2: Teams and data

  • Teams: set the number of teams (2 to 20), choose My Team so recommendations fit your roster, and edit team names. The number beside each name is its draft slot. The team count locks once picks are made.
  • Player data: shows how old your data is and lets you refresh it. Refreshing downloads the latest rankings and clears your current draft, so the app asks you to confirm.
  • Alerts: set a draft date and time for a countdown, and turn on draft day reminders. Android may ask for notification permission.
  • SMS Draft Updates: add US or Canada numbers (+1 and 10 digits). After each pick the app opens your messaging app with a pre-filled pick update for those numbers. You send the message.
  • About: the app version and a link to the release notes.

Step 3: Rules

  • Position Roster Requirements: the minimum and maximum for QB, RB, WR, TE, K and DST. They drive the Needs card and the recommendations.
  • Mock Draft: the AI picks for every team except yours. It needs My Team from step 2. You can also switch it on and off during the draft. Also set AI Difficulty (Easy (Loose), Normal or Hard (Tight)) and Busts (Skip Entirely or Penalize by 2 Rounds).
  • QB Premium (Superflex/2QB), Filter Injured from Best Available and Boost Favorites in Recommendations (about 1.5 to 2 rounds).
  • Draft Clock: an optional stopwatch for each pick, shown in the bar under the tabs.

Step 4: Draft order

  • Draft flow: Serpentine/Snake or Linear.
  • Keeper League Settings: linear rounds for keepers, from 0 (off) to 10. See Keepers.
  • Pick swaps and Trade picks: trade draft picks between teams. See Pick swaps.
  • Confirm the draft order: if you have staged swaps before the first pick, tap Confirm order first. Saving is blocked until you do.
  • Round 1 order: the first round listed in draft order, so you can check it before you start.
  • Save and Draft applies your settings and starts the draft.
Once picks exist, setup shows Draft in Progress - Configurations are locked down. Rounds, draft flow and keeper rounds are hidden. Team names, My Team, rules and unmade pick swaps stay available. Stop Draft & Reset ends the draft and clears all picks.

Best Available and recommendations

The card shows the player's photo, position rank, tier, health status, VBD, and ESPN and Depth links. Tap the tier button to see the tier list. The AI chip picks the player the recommendation engine likes best for the team on the clock. Its reasons can include:

  • Value: how far the player has fallen past ADP.
  • Scarcity: how few players remain at the position. It rises as the top players at a position are drafted.
  • Need: a roster slot your team has not filled.
  • Bye week: conflicts with players you already own.
  • Elite position rank, Favorite boost, TD Producer, yardage, Sleeper Alert and FLEX Value.

If Filter Injured from Best Available is on, players who are not healthy are skipped by the AI chip. They stay visible and draftable everywhere else.

Making and undoing picks

Make a pick

  • From Best Available: tap Draft This Player and confirm the pop-up.
  • From the Players tab: tap a player to open the Player Info card, then tap Draft.
  • A player who is not listed: use the add button on the Players tab and enter a name, position and NFL team.

With Mock Draft on, the AI picks for the other teams, and Draft is available only when your team is on the clock (the card then says Not your pick). With Mock Draft off, you record the pick for whichever team is on the clock, which suits a live draft with friends. During keeper rounds you make every pick yourself.

Undo a pick

Tap Undo under the Picks card (it appears after the first pick) and confirm. It removes the most recent pick and puts the player back on the board. Reset Draft clears every pick after a confirmation.

Pick swaps

Teams can trade draft picks one for one, one for many or many for many. Picks already made stay with the team that made them.

Before the draft

  1. Open setup step 4, Draft order, and find Trade picks.
  2. Choose the First team and Second team. Each shows the picks it holds, such as 1.03.
  3. Select the picks each team gives up, then tap Swap picks. A hint tells you when the selection is ready or cannot be swapped.
  4. The Swaps so far list shows each trade. Undo last swap removes the most recent one.
  5. Tap Confirm order to check Round 1 with swaps applied, then Save and Draft. Swaps take effect when you save.

During the draft

Open settings with the gear icon and go to step 4. You can trade any pick that has not been made yet. Undo last swap is available there too. The Draft screen updates to the new order when you save.

Changing the number of teams, rounds, draft flow or keeper rounds clears swaps that are not saved yet.

Traded picks show a ⇄ on the upcoming pick strip. On the Board, a yellow pill names the team that holds the pick, or the team that drafted it once it is made.

Player Info card and tiers

One card opens from everywhere: Best Available, the Players tab, the Picks list, History, the Board and tier lists. It shows:

  • The player's photo, position, NFL team and health status.
  • Stat boxes for ADP, VBD, Tier and Proj (projected points).
  • ESPN and Depth links.
  • A collapsible Stat breakdown with AI Draft Advice (tier, draft window, touchdowns, yardage and value).
  • AI top 5 for the team on the clock. Tap a name to switch the card to that player.
  • A Draft button when you can pick. For a drafted player the card says, for example, Drafted 2.03 by Team 3.

Tiers

Tap the tier box to see every player at that position, grouped by tier, with how many are left in each tier. LAST marks the final player in a tier, which is a good moment to act. Drafted players are marked. The Tier color guide link explains the colors: T1 is elite and green, deeper tiers shift toward red.

Players, Teams, Board and History

Players tab

  • Search by player, team or position.
  • Chips: Healthy (healthy players only), a position (All, QB, RB, WR, TE, K, DST) and Drafted (show drafted players, which are hidden by default).
  • Sort: Rank, ADP, VBD or Tier.
  • Favorite and bust tags: tap a player's photo to cycle favorite, bust, then no tag. The change saves right away and a message offers Undo. Favorites can be boosted in recommendations. Busts are skipped or pushed back according to your setup choice.

Teams tab

Pick My Team, On Clock or any team to see its roster grouped by position, position counts, a roster needs chart and each player's bye week (for example bye-7).

Board

A grid of every pick by round and team. Cells show the player, position rank, bye week and a health chip. Use Compact (it then reads Full) to fit more rounds on screen and Share to send the board as a CSV file. Tap a cell for player details. Traded picks carry the yellow ⇄ pill with the holder's name.

History

A list of every pick with round.pick, position, player name with the bye week next to it, health tag, value grade, NFL team, drafting team and overall number. Filter by team, flip the order with Sort, open the Board or Export the draft as CSV. Tap a row to open the Player Info card.

Finishing a draft and saving it

When the last pick is made, a confetti celebration plays and the Draft Grades screen opens with a grade for every team. There is no separate save prompt.

  1. Tap Save Draft on the Draft Grades screen. It appears once the draft is complete. Name the draft and tap Save.
  2. Tap any team to see its scorecard: letter grade, league placement, value and reach picks, and positional depth. Share Results exports it and View Draft shows the picks.

If you close the screen, open the three-dot menu and choose Analytics to bring it back. You can also open it during a draft to see a running grade, though Save Draft shows only after the draft is complete.

Saved drafts

Open the three-dot menu and choose Saved Drafts/Report Cards. The app keeps up to 50 drafts and replaces the oldest when it is full.

  • Board opens that draft's board.
  • Reports lets you pick a team and opens its report card, which you can share.
  • Delete removes one draft and Delete All removes everything. Both ask first and cannot be undone.
  • Saved drafts keep their keeper rounds and pick swaps, so boards and reports look right.

Backups

The app is set up for Android's automatic backup and device-to-device transfer. These are included: your settings, favorites and busts, saved drafts, saved trades, custom players, refreshed player data and the draft configuration backup. After a reinstall or a move to a new phone, the app restores your configuration and shows a short Restored config from backup message.

ESPN credentials are not included. Any ESPN login details stored on the device stay out of backups and transfers, so you would need to enter them again on a new device.

Whether a backup happens depends on your phone's Google backup setting.

Keepers

In setup step 4, set Linear Rounds under Keeper League Settings (0 turns it off, up to 10). Those first rounds use a linear order and you make every pick yourself, so you can enter each team's keepers. The AI does not pick in those rounds, even with Mock Draft on, and the Mock pill shows Keeper with the team name. Normal draft flow resumes after the last keeper round. Keeper rounds are saved with your draft, saved drafts and backups.

Release notes

See what changed in each version from the three-dot menu with App Change Log, or from setup step 2 under About with Release Notes.

Version 5.0.0.6

  • The tilted football icon that flashed before the splash screen on Android 12 and newer is gone. Only the splash screen shows.
  • The automatic Save Draft prompt after a draft completes was removed. Save from the Save Draft button on the Draft Grades screen.
  • Setup now sets teams in step 2 (Teams and data) and the mock draft and other rules in step 3 (Rules).

Version 5.0.0.5

  • Keeper rounds and pick swaps are saved with your draft, saved drafts and backups.
  • One bad saved value no longer drops your other settings or your saved drafts list.
  • ESPN login details are no longer included in backups or device transfers.
