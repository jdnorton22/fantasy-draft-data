1. Getting Started

FFL Draft Day Picker helps you make smarter fantasy football draft picks using AI-powered recommendations, real-time analytics, and ESPN player data.

First Launch Setup

  1. Open the app — you'll land on the Draft Screen
  2. Tap the ⚙️ gear icon (top right) to open Configuration
  3. Refresh Player Data first — this is at the top of the config screen
  4. Set your league name, number of teams, and rounds
  5. Open Teams settings to name your teams and select My Team
  6. Configure Position Requirements (min/max for each position)
  7. Tap "Save and Draft" to start
💡 Tip: Setting "My Team" is required for Mock Draft mode and enables personalized AI recommendations based on your roster.

5. AI Recommendation Engine

The AI evaluates every undrafted player across multiple dimensions to find your optimal pick.

Scoring Factors (Weighted Composite)

📈 Value (30%)

How far a player has fallen past their ADP. Bigger drop = better value.

⚡ Scarcity (25%)

Quality-weighted count of remaining players at that position. Includes run detection — if 3 QBs just went off the board, scarcity spikes.

🎯 Team Need (25%)

Whether your roster still needs that position based on min/max requirements.

📅 Bye Week (10%)

Penalizes players sharing bye weeks with your existing roster.

🏆 Elite (10%)

Bonus for top positional talent (top-5 for RB/WR/TE/K/DST, top-8 for QB as premium assets).

Additional Bonuses

  • ⭐ Favorites — +1.5 rounds worth of score boost
  • 🏈 TD Production (RB/WR/TE only):
    • 4+ TDs → +2 bonus
    • 6+ TDs → +3 bonus
    • 8+ TDs → +4 bonus
    • 10+ TDs → +5 bonus
  • 📈 Yardage — +1 for 1000+ yards, +2 for 1500+ yards (RB/WR/TE)
  • 🚨 Roster Urgency — Forces needed positions when rounds are running low

QB Premium Treatment NEW

Elite QBs are treated as premium assets:

  • Elite tier expanded to QB1–QB8 (other positions: top-5)
  • Scarcity floor: QB1-3 = 0.5 minimum, QB4-6 = 0.35 minimum
  • Prevents elite QBs from being buried by positional abundance

Position Run Detection NEW

The AI detects when multiple teams draft the same position in quick succession:

  • 2 picks at same position in recent round → +0.15 scarcity boost
  • 3 picks → +0.25 boost
  • 4+ picks → +0.35 boost

What Gets Skipped

  • 💣 Busts — Never recommended
  • Maxed positions — Skipped when roster max is reached
  • Injured players — -15 point penalty (shown, but deprioritized)

6. Mock Draft Mode

Simulate a full draft where AI picks for all teams except yours.

Setup

  1. Set My Team in Teams settings
  2. Enable Mock Draft in configuration (or tap the MOCK badge on the Mock tab)
  3. The AI will auto-pick for other teams at 500ms intervals

During Mock Draft

  • MOCK ON/OFF badge — Tap to toggle mock draft on/off mid-draft
  • "Your Pick!" pulse — Orange animated indicator when it's your turn
  • "Team's Pick (AI)" — Purple indicator when AI is picking
  • Draft button is disabled during AI turns
  • Stopwatch resets after each AI pick so your timer is accurate
💡 Tip: The AI mock draft uses the same recommendation engine — it respects position requirements, detects runs, and values TDs just like the recommendations you see.

8. Favorites & Busts

Access via the drawer menu → Favorites/Busts.

Favorites ⭐

  • Mark players you want to target
  • AI gives favorites a +1.5 round boost in recommendations
  • Highlighted with gold accent in the Favorites screen
  • Shows ⭐ next to name throughout the app

Busts 💣

  • Mark players you want to avoid
  • AI completely skips busts — never recommended
  • Highlighted with red accent
  • Shows 💣 next to name throughout the app
⚠️ Note: A player can be either a Favorite or a Bust, not both. Setting one clears the other.

9. Draft Grades & Analytics

Available during and after the draft — tap Analytics in the recent picks section.

Grade Report Includes

  • Overall score (uncapped — can exceed 100 for exceptional drafts)
  • Letter grade (A+ through F)
  • Draft strategy detection (RB Heavy, Balanced, Zero RB, etc.)
  • Value picks and reach picks count
  • Average ADP difference
  • Health risk assessment
  • Best/worst picks
  • Position Distribution bar chart
  • Positional Depth color-coded quality chart

Grading Modes

  • Curve — Grades relative to other teams in the same draft
  • Scale — Absolute thresholds (multiple teams can earn the same grade)

Set in Configuration under "Draft Grade Mode".

💡 Tip: The score is uncapped — following AI recommendations consistently can push your score above 100, reflecting truly exceptional drafting. Use this to compare how well you drafted across multiple sessions.

11. Understanding Scores

ESPN Rank

Expert consensus ranking from ESPN. Lower = better. Used as the default sort order.

ADP (Average Draft Position)

Where the player is typically drafted across all leagues. Drafting a player later than their ADP = value pick. Earlier = reach.

VBD (Value Based Drafting)

Measures how much better a player is than the "replacement level" at their position.

  • High VBD (30+) — Significantly better than waiver alternatives
  • Low VBD (1-10) — Marginally better than replacement
  • VBD 0 — At or below replacement level

AI Composite Score

The AI's overall rating combining all factors. Displayed on the player detail card with a full breakdown. Scale is 0–100+ (uncapped for exceptional values).

Draft Grade Score

Your team's overall draft quality. Base of 70, with bonuses for value picks and balanced roster, penalties for reaches and injuries. Uncapped — scores above 100 indicate exceptional drafting.

13. Pro Tips

  • Use AI mode as default — It accounts for value, scarcity, team need, bye weeks, elite talent, TD production, and position runs simultaneously
  • Watch for QB premium — The AI now properly values elite QBs. Don't let a QB6 slide if the AI recommends it
  • Monitor position runs — If you see 3 RBs go in a row, the AI's scarcity boost will kick in on your next RB-eligible pick
  • Mark busts early — Players you don't want will never pollute your recommendations
  • Tap recent pick cards — Quick access to full player stats, ESPN profile, and depth chart without leaving the draft screen
  • Use the Draft Board for context — See the full board to identify team needs and anticipate what positions opponents will target
  • Check the Top 5 — The AI Top 5 on detail cards lets you compare alternatives quickly
  • Use Mock Draft to practice — Run full mock drafts to test different strategies before draft day
  • Monitor Draft Grades live — Check analytics mid-draft to see how you're performing
  • Refresh player data on draft day — Pull latest rankings and injury status the morning of your draft
  • Score above 100? — That means you consistently found value. The uncapped scoring lets you track improvement across drafts
