1. Getting Started

FFL Draft Day Picker helps you make smarter fantasy football draft picks using AI-powered recommendations, real-time analytics, and ESPN player data.

First Launch Setup

  1. Open the app — you'll land on the Draft Screen
  2. Tap the ⚙️ gear icon (top right) to open Draft Configuration
  3. Set your league name, number of teams, and rounds
  4. Open Teams settings to name your teams and select My Team
  5. Configure Position Requirements (min/max for each position)
  6. Tap "Save and Draft" to start
💡 Tip: Setting "My Team" is required for Mock Draft mode and enables the AI to give you personalized recommendations based on your roster.

2. Draft Screen

The main draft screen shows everything you need during your draft:

Best Available Card

The top card shows the AI's recommended player with:

  • Player avatar with position-colored ring
  • Position rank under the avatar (e.g., RB4)
  • Team + bye week next to the name (e.g., ATL bye-11)
  • Rankings line — ESPN Rank, ADP, and VBD scores
  • Value grade — Good Value, Slight Reach, or Reach
  • AI reasoning bullets explaining why this player is recommended

Filter Buttons

Filter the Best Available by position:

🤖 AI Overall · Fav · QB RB WR TE K DST

Action Buttons

  • 👤 Player Info — Opens the full player detail card
  • Draft This Player — Drafts the displayed player
  • View Board — Opens the full player selection list
  • Reset Draft — Clears all picks and starts over

Recent Picks

Shows the last 3 picks with player avatars, team info, ESPN rank, ADP, and value indicators.

3. AI Recommendation Engine

The AI evaluates every undrafted player across multiple dimensions to find your optimal pick.

Scoring Factors

📈 Value (30%)

How far a player has fallen past their ADP. Bigger drop = better value.

⚡ Scarcity (25%)

How few players remain at that position. Fewer available = more scarce.

🎯 Team Need (25%)

Whether your roster still needs that position based on min/max requirements.

📅 Bye Week (10%)

Penalizes players sharing bye weeks with your existing roster.

🏆 Elite (10%)

Bonus for top-5 positional talent (e.g., RB1, WR2).

Additional Bonuses

  • ⭐ Favorites — +1.5 rounds worth of score boost
  • 🏈 TD Production — Small bonus for RB/WR/TE with high touchdown counts
  • 📈 Yardage — Bonus for 1000+ and 1500+ total yard producers
  • 🚨 Roster Urgency — Forces needed positions when rounds are running low

What Gets Skipped

  • 💣 Busts — Never recommended
  • Maxed positions — Skipped when roster max is reached
  • Injured players — -15 point penalty (still shown, but deprioritized)

4. Mock Draft Mode

Simulate a full draft where AI picks for all teams except yours.

Setup

  1. Set My Team in Teams settings
  2. Enable Mock Draft in configuration (or tap the MOCK badge on draft screen)
  3. The AI will auto-pick for other teams at 500ms intervals

During Mock Draft

  • MOCK ON/OFF badge — Tap to toggle mock draft on/off mid-draft
  • "Your Pick!" — Orange indicator when it's your turn
  • "Team's Pick (AI)" — Purple indicator when AI is picking
  • Draft button is disabled during AI turns
💡 Tip: The AI mock draft respects position requirements — it will fill QB, K, and DST slots as rounds run low, just like a real drafter would.

6. Favorites & Busts

Favorites ⭐

  • Mark players you want to target
  • AI gives favorites a +1.5 round boost in recommendations
  • Highlighted with gold accent bar in the Favorites screen
  • Shows ⭐ next to name throughout the app

Busts 💣

  • Mark players you want to avoid
  • AI completely skips busts — never recommended
  • Highlighted with red accent bar
  • Shows 💣 next to name throughout the app
⚠️ Note: A player can be either a Favorite or a Bust, not both. Setting one clears the other.

7. Draft Grades & Analytics

Available during and after the draft — tap the Analytics button once picks are made.

Grade Report Includes

  • Overall letter grade (A+ through F)
  • Draft strategy detection (RB Heavy, Balanced, Zero RB, etc.)
  • Value picks and reach picks count
  • Average ADP difference
  • Health risk assessment
  • Best/worst picks
  • Position Distribution — vertical bar chart
  • Positional Depth — horizontal bar chart with color-coded quality

Grading Modes

  • Curve — Grades relative to other teams (best gets A+, worst gets F)
  • Scale — Absolute thresholds (multiple teams can get the same grade)

Set in Draft Configuration under "Draft Grade Mode".

Depth Chart Legend

● 75+ Elite   ● 50-74 Solid   ● 25-49 Thin   ● <25 Weak

8. League Configuration

Access via the ⚙️ gear icon or hamburger menu → "Draft Configuration".

Settings

  • League Name
  • Number of Teams (2-20)
  • Number of Rounds (1-20)
  • Draft Flow — Serpentine or Linear
  • Keeper League — Linear rounds dropdown (0-10)
  • Player Sort Order — ESPN Expert Rank or ADP
  • Draft Grade Mode — Curve or Scale
  • Stopwatch — Pick timer
  • Mock Draft — AI auto-pick (requires My Team)
  • SMS Updates — Send pick notifications via SMS

Teams Settings

  • Name each team
  • Select My Team from dropdown (updates live as you type)
  • Team count locked during active draft

Position Requirements

  • Set Min and Max for each position (QB, RB, WR, TE, K, DST)
  • Tap ✕ to remove max limit
  • AI enforces these during recommendations and mock draft

Stop Draft & Reset

Red button appears when draft is in progress — stops mock draft and resets all picks.

9. Understanding Scores

ESPN Rank

Expert consensus ranking from ESPN. Lower = better. Used as the default sort order.

ADP (Average Draft Position)

Where the player is typically drafted across all leagues. Used to calculate value — if you draft a player later than their ADP, it's a value pick.

VBD (Value Based Drafting)

Measures how much better a player is than the "replacement level" at their position.

  • High VBD (30+) — Significantly better than waiver alternatives
  • Low VBD (1-10) — Marginally better than replacement
  • VBD 0 — At or below replacement level (common in later rounds)

VBD is most useful in early rounds where positional drop-off is steepest.

Composite Score (AI)

The AI's overall rating (0-100) combining all factors. Shown on the player detail card with a full breakdown of each factor's contribution.

10. Pro Tips

  • Use AI mode as default — It accounts for value, scarcity, team need, bye weeks, and elite talent simultaneously
  • Mark busts early — Players you don't want will never pollute your recommendations
  • Check the Top 5 — The AI Top 5 on the detail card lets you compare alternatives quickly
  • Watch the urgency bullets — 🚨 means you're running out of time to fill a position
  • Use Mock Draft to practice — Run full mock drafts to test different strategies
  • Monitor Draft Grades live — Check analytics mid-draft to see how you're doing
  • Refresh player data — Pull latest rankings/injury status before draft day
  • VBD for early rounds, AI for all rounds — VBD helps identify value in rounds 1-5; the AI handles the full picture
