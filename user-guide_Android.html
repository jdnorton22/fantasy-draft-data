1. 👋 Welcome / What is This App?

FFL Draft Day Picker is your real-time fantasy football draft companion for Android. It sits beside you during your live draft and provides AI-powered recommendations, value tracking, and team analytics — all in real time as picks happen.

This app is built for:

  • Fantasy football managers who want a competitive edge on draft day
  • Players in serpentine/snake or linear draft formats
  • Leagues with 4–16 teams and customizable roster requirements
  • Anyone who wants data-driven picks without memorizing spreadsheets

Whether you're a seasoned veteran or drafting for the first time, the app guides you through every pick with value-based analysis, positional scarcity awareness, and smart recommendations tailored to your roster needs.

2. 🚀 Getting Started

When you first launch the app, you'll configure your league settings:

First Launch Configuration

  • Number of Teams — Set how many teams are in your league (4–16)
  • Number of Rounds — How many rounds your draft will have
  • Flow Type — Choose Serpentine/Snake (draft order reverses each round) or Linear (same order every round)

Setting "My Team"

Tap the team selector to designate which draft position is yours. This is critical — it tells the AI which roster to optimize and highlights your pick slots in the draft timeline. The app tracks your roster needs and tailors recommendations specifically for your team.

Refreshing Player Data

The app pulls the latest player rankings, ADP, and projections from the cloud. Tap the refresh button to update player data anytime. Always refresh on the morning of your draft to get the most current rankings and injury statuses.

3. 📋 Understanding the Draft Screen

The draft screen is your command center during the draft. Here's what you'll see:

🏆 Best Available Card

At the top of the screen, the Best Available card displays the AI's top recommendation for the current pick. It shows:

  • Player name and position
  • Pick # vs ADP — Shows the current pick number compared to the player's Average Draft Position, so you can instantly see if you're getting value or reaching

Position Filter Pills

Below the Best Available card, you'll find position filter pills (QB, RB, WR, TE, K, DST). Tap any pill to filter the available players list by that position. Tap again to clear the filter.

Info Tabs

Three tabs let you switch between different views:

  • Pick — Shows available players and the AI recommendation
  • Roster — Displays the current team's roster as it fills up
  • Draft — Shows the full draft history of picks made so far

📍 Draft Timeline

The Draft Timeline shows recent picks plus 4 rounds of projected future picks. Your pick slots are highlighted in green so you can instantly see when you're up next and plan ahead. The 📍 focus button scrolls the timeline back to the current pick position if you've scrolled away.

On the Clock Card

Displays which team is currently picking with the team name prominently shown. When it's your team, it's highlighted so you never miss your turn.

Action Buttons

  • Analytics — View detailed pick analytics and value data
  • View All — See the full list of available players
  • Undo — Reverse the last pick (mistakes happen!)

4. ✅ Drafting Players

Drafting the AI Recommendation

The fastest way to pick: tap the Best Available card to draft the AI's top recommendation. The AI has already factored in your roster needs, value, and positional scarcity — so if you trust it, one tap and you're done.

Searching for a Specific Player

If you have a player in mind that differs from the AI pick, use the search function to find them by name. You can also scroll the available players list or use position filter pills to narrow down your options.

Value Indicators

Every player shows a value indicator comparing their ADP to the current pick number:

  • 🟣 Elite Value — Exceptional steal at this pick
  • 🟢 Great Value — Solid value, going later than expected
  • 🔵 Fair Value — Right around their expected draft position
  • 🟡 Slight Reach — Going a bit earlier than ADP suggests
  • 🔴 Big Reach — Significantly earlier than ADP, proceed with caution

⏱ Stopwatch Timer

Enable the stopwatch in Settings to track how long each pick takes. Useful for leagues with timed picks — it keeps you aware of the clock without needing a separate timer.

5. 🧠 AI Recommendations Explained

The AI engine isn't just picking the highest-ranked player available. It uses multiple factors to find the optimal pick for your team at this moment:

VBD (Value Based Drafting)

At its core, the AI uses VBD — Value Based Drafting. In simple terms, VBD measures how much better a player is compared to the replacement-level player at that position (the best player available on waivers after the draft). A QB projected for 350 points isn't as valuable as an RB projected for 250 points if the replacement QB scores 300 but the replacement RB only scores 100.

Positional Scarcity

The AI tracks how quickly positions are being drafted and adjusts recommendations when certain positions are drying up. If starting-caliber RBs are disappearing fast, the AI may bump RB priority before it's too late.

QB Premium Mode

For Superflex or 2QB leagues, enable QB Premium in Settings. This inflates QB VBD values to reflect their true scarcity and scoring advantage in leagues where QBs are premium assets.

Sleeper Alerts

The AI identifies sleeper candidates — players whose projections significantly exceed their ADP, representing potential breakout value.

TD Producer Flags

Players with high projected touchdown volume are flagged, since TDs are often the difference between a good week and a great one.

Favorites & Busts Influence

Your personal tags directly influence the AI:

  • Favorites get a +5 VBD boost, making them more likely to be recommended
  • Busts are skipped entirely by the AI — it will never recommend a player you've tagged as a bust

Proactive Need Awareness

The AI is proactive, not reactive. It won't defer filling positions until you're in a panic late in the draft. It monitors your roster gaps and starts addressing needs early when quality options are still available.

Reach Penalty

The AI won't reach 2+ rounds beyond ADP just to fill a positional need. If the only way to fill a position is a massive reach, it will take the better value player and address the need in a later round when the price is more reasonable.

6. 🤖 Mock Draft Mode

Mock Draft Mode lets you practice your draft strategy before the real thing. When enabled:

  • The AI auto-picks for all other teams, simulating realistic draft behavior
  • When it's your team's turn, you'll see the "Your Pick" indicator and can make your selection manually
  • Requires "My Team" to be set — the AI needs to know which team you're controlling

Use Mock Draft Mode to test different strategies, see how the AI values fall, and build confidence in your approach before draft day. You can run as many mock drafts as you like and save them to compare results.

7. 🔒 Keeper / Linear Rounds

What Are Keepers?

In keeper leagues, teams retain select players from the previous season. These players are "kept" in specific rounds, meaning those draft slots are pre-filled and not available for regular picks.

Configuring Forced Linear Rounds

Some leagues use keeper rounds that operate in linear order (same pick order as Round 1) regardless of whether the rest of the draft is serpentine. In Settings, you can configure specific rounds as forced linear — the app will adjust the draft flow so those rounds don't follow the serpentine reversal pattern.

This is perfect for leagues where keeper rounds are "straight" picks while the remaining rounds snake normally.

8. 📊 Draft Board

The Draft Board provides a full grid view of the entire draft — every pick, every team, every round laid out visually.

  • Color-coded by position — Each position (QB, RB, WR, TE, K, DST) has its own distinct color, making it easy to spot roster construction patterns at a glance
  • Tap any cell for player detail including their VBD score and value tier (Elite/Great/Fair/Reach)
  • See which teams loaded up on certain positions early vs. late
  • Identify league trends and potential trade targets

The board scrolls horizontally and vertically to accommodate any league size. It's your bird's-eye view of the entire draft.

9. 🔍 Player Search

The Player Search screen gives you full access to the player database:

  • Search bar at top — Type any player name to find them instantly
  • Position filter pills — Tap QB, RB, WR, TE, K, or DST to filter by position
  • Hide Drafted toggle — Switch on to hide players already drafted, showing only available players

Player Detail Cards

Tap any player to view their detail card, which includes:

  • Full player stats and projections
  • VBD score and value tier
  • ADP and overall rank
  • ESPN link — Jump directly to the player's ESPN profile
  • Depth Chart link — View the player's team depth chart to check their role

10. ⚖️ Trade Analyzer

The Trade Analyzer helps you evaluate potential trades during or after the draft.

How It Works

  • Giving side — Add the players you would trade away
  • Receiving side — Add the players you would get back

8-Factor Comparison

The analyzer evaluates trades across 8 factors to give you a comprehensive picture of trade value, including VBD, positional value, projected points, and more.

Action Buttons

  • Save — Store the trade for later review
  • Clear — Reset both sides and start fresh
  • Delete — Remove a saved trade
  • 🗂 Saved — Access your previously saved trade analyses

Verdict Interpretation

After analysis, you'll receive a verdict indicating whether the trade favors you, favors the other side, or is roughly even. Use this as a guide — context like roster needs and league dynamics matter too, but the numbers give you a solid baseline for negotiation.

11. ⭐ Favorites & Busts

Tag players before or during the draft to personalize the AI's behavior:

Tagging Players

  • Favorite — Players you're targeting. They receive a +5 VBD boost in AI calculations.
  • Bust — Players you want to avoid. The AI will completely skip them when making recommendations.

Filter Options

The Favorites & Busts screen provides powerful filtering:

  • Type filters (highlighted in blue when active): All Players / Favorites / Busts / Both
  • Position filters (highlighted in green when active): Any / QB / RB / WR / TE / K / DST

How Tags Affect the AI

Tags are applied globally — once you tag a player, the AI immediately adjusts. Favorites bubble up in recommendations while Busts are excluded entirely. This is your way of injecting personal knowledge (insider reports, gut feelings, podcast intel) into the algorithm.

12. 💾 Saved Drafts & Report Cards

Access your saved drafts from the navigation drawer → Saved Drafts/Report Cards. The screen provides full-screen management of your draft history.

Draft Management

Each saved draft shows the save time and has three action buttons:

  • 🟢 Board (green) — Open the full draft board for this draft
  • 🔵 Reports (blue) — View the report card and grade
  • 🔴 Delete (red) — Remove this draft permanently

Sort drafts by newest or oldest. The app stores up to 30 drafts maximum.

Report Card Sections

Each report card includes:

  • Grade — Displayed as a ring visualization with your letter grade
  • League Comparison — Rank badge showing your position plus a vs. average table comparing your team to the league average
  • Positional Depth — Bar charts showing your depth at each position
  • Strengths & Weaknesses — AI-generated analysis of your roster's strong and weak points
  • Bye Week Map — Visual display of your players' bye weeks to identify potential trouble weeks
  • Pick-by-Pick — Clickable list of every pick you made, tap any for full player detail
  • Full Roster — Complete roster view organized by position

Export as JPEG

Tap the blue download FAB (floating action button) on the report card screen to export your report card as a JPEG image directly to your device gallery (saved to Pictures/DraftExports). Share it with your league mates to show off — or keep it as a receipt.

FAB Navigation

Use the floating action button to quickly navigate between report card sections without scrolling through the entire report.

13. 📈 Draft Grading Explained

Important: The draft grade is now 100% based on total VBD (Value Based Drafting) score.

How It Works

Your grade is determined by the sum of VBD values across all your drafted players. The team with the highest total VBD across their entire roster earns the highest raw score. It's simple: draft players who provide the most value above replacement level, and your grade goes up.

Grading Modes

  • Curve Mode — Normalizes grades across all teams in the league. The team with the highest total VBD gets an A+, the lowest gets an F, and everyone else is distributed between. This is relative grading — your grade depends on how you compare to other teams.
  • Scale Mode — Uses your raw VBD total directly against a fixed scale. Your grade is independent of how other teams drafted.

Letter Grade Scale

  • A+ — 90 and above
  • A — 85–89
  • A- — 80–84
  • B+ — 75–79
  • B — 70–74
  • B- — 65–69
  • C+ — 60–64
  • C — 55–59
  • C- — 50–54
  • F — Below 50

What VBD Means for Your Grade

VBD measures how much value a player provides above the replacement level at their position. Drafting high-VBD players means you're getting more production above what's freely available — and that directly translates to a better grade. Focus on value, not just big names.

  • Rounds — Number of draft rounds

15. 💡 Tips for Draft Day

  1. Refresh data the morning of your draft — Get the latest rankings, injury updates, and ADP shifts before you go live
  2. Set "My Team" before the draft starts — The AI can't help you if it doesn't know which team is yours
  3. Use the AI as a guide, not gospel — The recommendations are data-driven and strong, but you know your league. If your league mates always reach for QBs early, adjust accordingly
  4. Watch bye weeks — Use the report card's Bye Week Map to avoid stacking too many players on the same bye
  5. Tag favorites and busts before the draft — Do your research ahead of time and tag players. This personalizes the AI and saves decision time during the draft
  6. Save your draft when complete — Tap save to preserve your full draft board and generate a report card for post-draft analysis
  7. Use the timeline to plan ahead — Check what's projected at your next pick slot. If a top RB might fall to your next turn, maybe you grab a WR now instead

16. ❓ FAQ

"Why is my grade low?"

Your draft grade is 100% based on total VBD. If your grade seems low in Curve mode, remember that AI-controlled teams (in mock drafts) also draft high-VBD players efficiently. The curve places you relative to all teams — so even a solid draft might grade as a B if other teams also drafted well. Check your VBD total vs. the league average in the League Comparison section to see where you stand.

"Where are my saved drafts?"

Open the navigation drawer (hamburger menu) → tap Saved Drafts/Report Cards. All your saved drafts are there, sorted by date.

"Can I undo multiple picks?"

Yes! You can undo picks one at a time. Tap the Undo button repeatedly to step back through multiple picks. Each undo reverses the most recent pick.

"What does VBD mean?"

VBD stands for Value Based Drafting. It measures how much value a player provides above the replacement-level player at the same position. A replacement-level player is essentially the best player you could get for free off waivers. Higher VBD = more value above what's freely available.

"How do I export my report card?"

On the report card screen, tap the blue download FAB (floating action button). The report card is saved as a JPEG image to your device at Pictures/DraftExports. From there you can share it via any app.

"What is QB Premium?"

QB Premium mode inflates QB VBD values to account for Superflex or 2QB league formats where quarterbacks are significantly more valuable than in standard 1QB leagues. Enable this in Settings if your league starts 2 QBs or has a Superflex slot.

"Why does the timeline show my pick in green?"

Green-highlighted slots in the Draft Timeline are your projected draft picks based on the serpentine order and your team position. This lets you see exactly when your next picks are coming so you can plan ahead and anticipate which players might still be available.
