👋Welcome / What is This App?

Welcome to FFL Draft Day Picker — your real-time fantasy football draft companion. This app sits beside you during your live draft, tracking every pick as it happens, recommending the best available players using AI-powered analysis, and grading your completed draft so you know exactly where you stand.

What It Does

  • 📊 Tracks picks in real time — logs every selection across all teams as your draft unfolds
  • 🤖 Recommends players — uses Value Based Drafting (VBD) and positional scarcity to suggest your best pick
  • 📝 Grades your draft — gives you a full report card with letter grade, strengths, weaknesses, and comparisons
  • 🔄 Analyzes trades — evaluates potential trades across 8 factors so you can deal with confidence

Who Is This For?

Anyone participating in a fantasy football draft — whether it's a snake/serpentine draft (where pick order reverses each round) or a linear draft (same order every round). Whether you're a first-time player or a seasoned veteran looking for a data edge, this app has you covered.

New to fantasy football? A "draft" is where each team in your league takes turns selecting NFL players. Your goal is to build the best roster by picking players who will score the most points throughout the season.

🚀Getting Started

First Launch: Configure Your League

When you first open the app, you'll need to set up your league configuration so the app matches your draft format:

  1. Team Count — How many teams are in your league (typically 8, 10, 12, or 14)
  2. Rounds — How many rounds your draft will have (usually 15-18)
  3. Draft Flow Type — Choose between:
    • Snake/Serpentine — Pick order reverses each round (most common format)
    • Linear — Same pick order every round

Setting Your Team Name and "My Team"

Tell the app which draft position is yours by setting "My Team." This enables personalized recommendations and features like Mock Draft mode. You can also enter your team name for a customized experience.

Setting "My Team" is required for Mock Draft mode and for getting personalized AI recommendations that account for your current roster.

Refreshing Player Data

Player rankings, ADP (Average Draft Position), and injury data are updated regularly. Before draft day, tap the refresh/update option to pull the latest player data. This ensures you're working with the most current rankings and projections.

Always refresh your player data the morning of your draft. Player injuries or depth chart changes can happen at any time.

📱Understanding the Draft Screen

The draft screen is your command center during the live draft. Here's what each element does:

🎯 Best Available Player Card

At the top of the screen, you'll see the app's #1 AI recommendation — the player it believes offers the best value at this moment. The card shows the player's name, team, position, and value indicator. This updates automatically after every pick.

🏷️ Position Filter Pills

Below the recommendation, you'll find position filter buttons:

  • WR — Wide Receivers

Tap a pill to filter the best available list to just that position. Tap again to clear the filter.

📑 Info Tabs (Pick, Roster, Draft)

  • Pick Tab — Shows the current pick number, round, and which team is on the clock
  • Roster Tab — Displays your team's current roster as you build it
  • Draft Tab — Shows the full draft order and upcoming picks

📜 Recent Picks Strip

A horizontal scrolling strip showing the last several picks made in the draft. Keeps you aware of what just happened without leaving the main screen.

🔘 Action Buttons

  • Analytics — Opens detailed positional analysis and draft trends
  • View All — Shows the complete list of available players
  • Undo — Reverses the most recent pick (mistakes happen!)

✋Drafting Players

Drafting the AI Recommendation

The fastest way to make a pick: simply tap the Best Available player card at the top of the screen. The app will record that player as the current pick and advance to the next team.

Searching for a Specific Player

If someone in your draft selects a player who isn't the AI's top recommendation (or you want to pick someone specific):

  1. Use the search feature to find the player by name
  2. Tap the player to select them
  3. Confirm the pick

Understanding Value Indicators

Every player displays a value badge indicating how their draft position compares to their projected value:

  • ELITE — Exceptional value; this player is ranked significantly higher than where they're being drafted
  • GREAT VALUE — Solid pick; you're getting more value than expected at this position
  • FAIR — Reasonable pick; the player is going right around their expected draft position
  • REACH — You're drafting this player earlier than their ranking suggests; they might be available later

⏱️ The Stopwatch Timer

An optional pick timer that tracks how long each selection takes. Useful for leagues with time limits on picks. Enable it in Settings to keep your draft moving.

🤖AI Recommendations Explained

What is VBD (Value Based Drafting)?

VBD is the brain behind the app's recommendations. In simple terms, it measures how much better a player is compared to the last starter at their position. A quarterback who scores 350 points sounds great — but if the 12th-best QB scores 300, that's only 50 points of value. A running back scoring 250 points when the last starting RB scores 150 gives you 100 points of extra value. VBD helps you focus on players who give you the biggest advantage over replacement-level options.

How Positional Scarcity Works

The app tracks how many players remain at each position and adjusts recommendations accordingly. If running backs are flying off the board and the drop-off at RB is steep, the app will push RBs higher in its rankings — even if a WR has slightly better raw value. This prevents you from ending up with thin rosters at critical positions.

QB Premium Mode (Superflex/2QB Leagues)

If your league uses a Superflex or 2QB format, enable QB Premium in Settings. This significantly boosts quarterback values in the algorithm since QBs become much scarcer when every team needs two. The recommendations will push QBs earlier than in standard single-QB leagues.

🌟 Sleeper Alerts & TD Producer Flags

  • Sleeper Alert — Flags players whose ADP is much lower than their projected production. These are potential late-round steals.
  • TD Producer — Highlights players with high projected touchdown totals. Touchdowns are high-value scoring events and these players have extra upside.

How Favorites/Busts Influence Recommendations

Players you mark as Favorites get a boost in the AI's rankings, making them more likely to appear as the top recommendation. Players marked as Busts get penalized and will drop in the recommendations. This lets you layer your personal knowledge on top of the data.

🎮Mock Draft Mode

What It Does

Mock Draft mode lets you practice before the real thing. When enabled, the AI automatically makes picks for all other teams, simulating a realistic draft environment. You only need to make selections when it's your turn. This is perfect for testing strategies, seeing how players fall, and getting comfortable with the app's interface.

How to Enable It

  1. First, make sure you've set "My Team" in the configuration (this is required)
  2. Go to Settings and enable Mock Draft
  3. Start your draft — the app will auto-pick for other teams and pause when it's your turn

"Your Pick" Indicator

When Mock Draft is active, you'll see a clear "Your Pick" indicator when it's your turn to select. The app pauses the auto-drafting and waits for your input. Take your time, review the recommendations, and make your selection.

Use Mock Draft mode a few times before your real draft to get familiar with how the app works and to test different drafting strategies.

🔒Keeper / Linear Rounds

What Are Keeper Leagues?

In a keeper league, each team retains a set number of players from last year's roster before the draft begins. These "kept" players are typically assigned to specific early rounds. For example, if you keep a player, they might count as your 1st or 2nd round pick.

How to Configure Forced Linear Rounds

Many keeper leagues use linear pick order for the keeper rounds (since positions are pre-assigned) and then switch to snake format for the remaining rounds. The app supports this hybrid format:

  1. Go to Settings → Draft tab
  2. Find the Keeper Rounds setting
  3. Set the number of rounds that should be forced linear (e.g., 2 rounds means rounds 1-2 are linear, then snake begins in round 3)
If your league doesn't use keepers, leave this set to 0 and ignore this feature entirely.

📊Draft Board

Full Grid View

The Draft Board shows a complete grid of every pick in your draft — a bird's-eye view of how the entire draft unfolded. It's accessible from the navigation menu and is especially useful for spotting patterns and identifying team needs.

How to Read It

  • Columns represent each team in the league
  • Rows represent each round of the draft
  • Each cell shows the player selected in that slot (or is empty if the pick hasn't happened yet)
  • Players are color-coded by position for quick scanning

Tapping Picks for Detail

Tap any pick on the draft board to open a player detail card. You'll see their full stats, position ranking, ADP information, and more. This is a great way to review what other teams have done and assess their roster composition.

🔍Player Search

Search Bar and Position Filters

The Player Search screen gives you full access to the player database. Type a name in the search bar to find any player quickly. Combine text search with position filter pills to narrow results further (e.g., search "Smith" with the WR filter to find all wide receivers named Smith).

Hide Drafted Toggle

Enable the "Hide Drafted" toggle to remove all already-selected players from the list. This is the default during a live draft so you only see players still available.

Player Detail Cards

Tap any player to open their full detail card, which includes:

  • 📈 Projected stats — Points, touchdowns, yards
  • 🔗 ESPN link — Quick link to their ESPN player page for news and injury updates
  • 📋 Depth chart position — Where they stand on their team's depth chart
  • 📊 ADP and rank — How the community values them vs. expert rankings
  • 💰 VBD score — Their value-over-replacement metric

🔄Trade Analyzer

Adding Players to a Trade

The Trade Analyzer lets you evaluate potential deals before pulling the trigger. It has two sides:

  • Giving — Players you would trade away
  • Receiving — Players you would get back

Add players to each side by searching and selecting them. You can build multi-player trades for complex deals.

The 8-Factor Comparison

Once both sides have players, the analyzer evaluates the trade across 8 key factors including projected points, positional value, consistency, upside, injury risk, schedule strength, and more. Each factor shows which side "wins" that category.

Saving, Loading, and Deleting Scenarios

  • Save — Store a trade scenario to revisit later (great for evaluating offers over time)
  • Load — Pull up a previously saved trade to review or modify
  • Delete — Remove saved scenarios you no longer need

Verdict Interpretation

After analysis, you'll see a clear verdict:

  • ✅ Good Trade — The receiving side clearly wins; you should accept this deal
  • ⚖️ Even Trade — Both sides are roughly equal in value; consider your team needs
  • ❌ Bad Trade — The giving side loses value; consider rejecting or renegotiating

⭐Favorites & Busts

How to Tag Players

Before or during your draft, you can mark players with two special tags:

  • ⭐ Favorite — A player you want to target; someone you believe will outperform their ranking
  • 👎 Bust — A player you want to avoid; someone you think is overvalued or risky

Tag players by long-pressing or using the tag option in their player detail card.

Filter Options

On the player list, you can filter the view to:

  • All Players — Everyone (default view)
  • Favorites — Only your starred targets
  • Busts — Only your flagged avoids
  • Both — Shows players with either tag

How Tags Affect AI Recommendations

Your tags directly influence the AI. Favorites receive a ranking boost, making them appear as recommendations sooner. Busts receive a ranking penalty, pushing them lower in the suggested order. This combines the app's data-driven analysis with your personal expertise and league knowledge.

Tag your favorites and busts before draft day while doing your research. This front-loads your strategy into the app so you can move quickly during the live draft.

💾Saved Drafts & Report Cards

Saving a Completed Draft

When your draft is complete, save it for future reference. The app will store the entire draft board, all team rosters, and generate comprehensive report cards. You can save multiple drafts (great for mock drafts and your actual draft).

Viewing Saved Drafts

Access saved drafts from the navigation drawer menu. Each saved draft gives you three options:

  • 📊 Board — View the full draft board grid
  • 📝 Reports — View team report cards and analysis
  • 🗑️ Delete — Remove a saved draft

Team Report Card Sections

The report card is a comprehensive analysis of any team's draft. It includes:

  • 🎓 Overall Grade — Letter grade (A+ through F) with numeric score
  • 📊 League Comparison — How the team ranks against all other teams in the draft
  • 📋 Positional Depth — Breakdown of players at each position and depth chart strength
  • 💪 Strengths — What the team did well (e.g., "Excellent RB depth")
  • ⚠️ Weaknesses — Areas of concern (e.g., "No backup TE")
  • 📅 Bye Week Map — Visual calendar showing when your players have bye weeks and potential problem weeks
  • 🔢 Pick-by-Pick Analysis — Value assessment of each individual selection
  • 📋 Full Roster — Complete team listing organized by position

Exporting Report Card as Image

Want to share your grade with your league? Use the export/share button (floating action button) to save your report card as an image. Perfect for sending to your group chat to show off an A+ draft (or roast a friend's C- draft).

Back Navigation (FAB)

Use the floating action button (FAB) for quick navigation back to the main saved drafts list when you're deep in a report card view.

🎓Draft Grading Explained

How the Grade is Calculated

Your draft grade starts with a base score of 70 and is adjusted based on the quality of your picks:

Factor Impact
Value picks (drafted below ADP) +3 per pick
Reaches (drafted above ADP) -2 per pick
Average ADP difference (all picks) +1 per avg point of value
Elite players acquired +3 per elite pick
Injured players drafted -1.5 per injured player
Balanced roster composition +3 bonus

Curve vs Scale Grading Modes

  • Curve Mode — Grades are relative to other teams in your draft. If everyone reaches, the curve softens the penalty. This mimics a "class curve" and ensures grades feel fair within your league's context.
  • Scale Mode — Grades are based on an absolute standard. A score is a score regardless of how other teams drafted. This is more objective but can feel harsh in competitive leagues where everyone drafts well.

💡Tips for Draft Day

  1. 🔄 Refresh player data the morning of your draft
    Player news changes fast. A last-minute injury can shake up the entire board. Get the freshest data possible.
  2. 👤 Set your team before starting
    Configure "My Team" so the app knows which picks are yours and can personalize recommendations to your roster.
  3. 🤖 Use the AI recommendation as a guide, not gospel
    The AI is data-driven but doesn't know your league's tendencies, inside jokes, or homer picks. Trust the data but trust your gut when you have strong conviction.
  4. 📅 Watch the bye week distribution
    Having 4 starters on the same bye week creates a headache. The report card tracks this, but be mindful during the draft too.
  5. ⭐ Tag favorites and busts before the draft
    Do your research early and tag players. This pre-loads your strategy into the algorithm and saves time during fast-moving picks.
  6. 💾 Save your draft when complete
    Always save your finished draft for the report card, grade, and future reference. You'll want to look back at it during the season.

❓FAQ

Q: "Why is my draft grade low?"

The grade is based primarily on value relative to ADP — not player talent alone. If you draft a great player 20 picks earlier than their ADP, that's a "reach" even if the player is talented. The grade measures whether you could have gotten the same players later and picked up additional value. Think of it as draft efficiency, not necessarily roster quality.

Q: "Where are my saved drafts?"

Open the navigation drawer menu (swipe from the left edge or tap the hamburger menu icon) and look for the "Saved Drafts" option. All your saved drafts are stored there with options to view the board, reports, or delete.

Q: "Can I undo multiple picks?"

Yes! You can undo picks one at a time. Each tap of the Undo button reverses the most recent pick. Keep tapping to undo multiple picks in reverse order. This is handy for correcting input mistakes during a live draft.

Q: "What does VBD mean?"

Value Based Drafting (VBD) is a method for comparing players across different positions. It measures how much better a player is than the baseline player at their position (usually the last starter). A high VBD means the player gives you a big advantage over what's freely available. It's the fairest way to compare, say, a QB to a RB, since their raw point totals are very different.

Q: "How do I share my report card?"

Open your saved draft's report card, then tap the floating action button (FAB) — the circular button at the bottom of the screen. Select the export/share option to save the report as an image file you can send via text, email, or post to your league's group chat.
