1. 👋 Welcome / What is This App?

FFL Draft Day Picker is your real-time fantasy football draft companion for Android. It sits beside you during your live draft and provides AI-powered recommendations, value tracking, and team analytics — all in real time as picks happen.

This app is built for:

  • Fantasy football managers who want a competitive edge on draft day
  • Players in serpentine/snake or linear draft formats
  • Leagues with 4–16 teams and customizable roster requirements
  • Standard, Half-PPR, and Full PPR scoring formats
  • Anyone who wants data-driven picks without memorizing spreadsheets

Whether you're a seasoned veteran or drafting for the first time, the app guides you through every pick with value-based analysis, positional scarcity awareness, and smart recommendations tailored to your roster needs.

2. 🚀 Getting Started

When you first launch the app, you'll configure your league settings:

First Launch Configuration

  • Number of Teams — Set how many teams are in your league (4–16)
  • Number of Rounds — How many rounds your draft will have
  • Flow Type — Choose Serpentine/Snake (draft order reverses each round) or Linear (same order every round)
  • Scoring Format — Standard, Half PPR, or Full PPR

Setting "My Team"

Tap the team selector to designate which draft position is yours. This is critical — it tells the AI which roster to optimize and highlights your pick slots in the draft timeline. The app tracks your roster needs and tailors recommendations specifically for your team.

Refreshing Player Data

The app pulls the latest player rankings, ADP, and projections from the cloud. Tap the refresh button to update player data anytime. Always refresh on the morning of your draft to get the most current rankings and injury statuses.

3. 📋 Understanding the Draft Screen

The draft screen is your command center during the draft. Here's what you'll see:

🏆 Best Available Card

At the top of the screen, the Best Available card displays the AI's top recommendation for the current pick. It shows:

  • Player name and position
  • Pick # vs ADP — Shows the current pick number compared to the player's Average Draft Position, so you can instantly see if you're getting value or reaching

Tap the card to open the full Player Detail Card with rankings, last year stats, projected points (with ℹ️ breakdown link), AI composite score pie chart, and Draft Advisor analysis.

Position Filter Pills

Below the Best Available card, you'll find position filter pills (QB, RB, WR, TE, K, DST). Tap any pill to filter the available players list by that position. Tap again to clear the filter.

Info Tabs

Three tabs let you switch between different views:

  • Pick — Shows available players and the AI recommendation
  • Roster — Displays the current team's roster as it fills up
  • Draft — Shows the full draft history of picks made so far, color scheme toggle, and mock draft settings

📍 Draft Timeline

The Draft Timeline shows recent picks plus 4 rounds of projected future picks. Your pick slots are highlighted in green so you can instantly see when you're up next and plan ahead. The ⊕ focus button scrolls the timeline back to the current pick position if you've scrolled away. Scrolling manually disables auto-focus until you tap the focus button again.

On the Clock Card

Displays which team is currently picking with the team name prominently shown. When it's your team, it's highlighted so you never miss your turn.

Action Buttons

  • Analytics — View detailed pick analytics and value data
  • View Board — Open the full draft board grid
  • Undo — Reverse the last pick (mistakes happen!)

4. ✅ Drafting Players

Drafting the AI Recommendation

The fastest way to pick: tap the Best Available card to open the player detail, then tap "Draft." The AI has already factored in your roster needs, value, and positional scarcity — so if you trust it, two taps and you're done.

Player Detail Card

When you tap any player, the detail card shows:

  • Player avatar with position-colored ring
  • Rankings — ADP, Position Rank, ESPN Rank, VBD score
  • Last year stats — Full stat line from previous season
  • Projected Points — Calculated from stats with ℹ️ tap for full breakdown (yards × rate, TDs × rate, receptions for PPR)
  • Injury status — Color-coded health indicator
  • Draft Advisor — Contextual analysis with Franchise tag for top-3 positional players
  • AI Composite Score — Pie chart showing Value, Scarcity, Need, Bye, and Elite factors
  • AI Top 5 — See how this player ranks among the AI's top recommendations
  • ESPN link and Depth Chart buttons

Value Indicators

Every player shows a value indicator comparing their ADP to the current pick number:

  • 🟣 Elite Value — Exceptional steal at this pick
  • 🟢 Great Value — Solid value, going later than expected
  • 🔵 Fair Value — Right around their expected draft position
  • 🟡 Slight Reach — Going a bit earlier than ADP suggests
  • 🔴 Big Reach — Significantly earlier than ADP, proceed with caution

⏱ Stopwatch Timer

Enable the stopwatch in Settings to track how long each pick takes. Useful for leagues with timed picks.

5. 🧠 AI Recommendations Explained

The AI engine uses a weighted composite scoring system across five dimensions to find the optimal pick for your team at this moment:

VBD (Value Based Drafting) — 30% Weight

At its core, the AI uses VBD. It measures how much better a player is compared to the replacement-level player at that position. A QB projected for 350 points isn't as valuable as an RB projected for 250 points if the replacement QB scores 300 but the replacement RB only scores 100. High-VBD players get up to +15 bonus points in the composite score.

Positional Scarcity — 25% Weight

The AI tracks how quickly positions are being drafted and adjusts when certain positions are drying up. It uses quality-weighted scarcity (only counts startable-depth players, not deep bench fillers) and detects position runs (multiple teams drafting the same position in quick succession).

Team Need — 25% Weight

Your roster requirements drive this factor. The AI monitors unfilled position minimums and provides urgency boosts as rounds run out. It's proactive — gently nudging toward needs before the crunch, not waiting until panic time.

Bye Week — 10% Weight

The AI avoids stacking too many of your players on the same bye week, reducing the risk of a catastrophic zero-point week during the fantasy season.

Elite Factor — 10% Weight

Top-ranked positional players receive an elite bonus. The AI recognizes that the difference between RB1 and RB15 is massive compared to the difference between RB15 and RB30.

PPR-Aware Scoring

In Half-PPR and Full PPR leagues, the AI boosts high-reception players:

  • 90+ receptions last year: significant boost
  • 70–89 receptions: moderate boost
  • 50–69 receptions: slight boost

This means pass-catching RBs and high-target WRs/TEs are properly valued in PPR formats.

FLEX Slot Awareness

The AI factors in FLEX roster positions (RB/WR/TE eligible). When FLEX slots need filling, it boosts the depth of flex-eligible players and gives extra credit to pass-catching RBs who can fill multiple roles.

Franchise Tag

The Draft Advisor highlights the top 3 players at QB, RB, WR, and TE with a 🏷️ Franchise tag designation, indicating elite positional talent worth prioritizing.

QB Premium Mode

For Superflex or 2QB leagues, enable QB Premium in Settings. This inflates QB values significantly — QB1-3 get first-round caliber boosts, QB4-8 get mid-round boosts.

Additional AI Features

  • Sleeper Alerts — Flags players whose ADP is 20+ picks below their rank with proven production (💤)
  • TD Producer Flags — Tiered bonuses for players with 4+ TDs last season (🏈)
  • Reach Penalty — Won't reach 2+ rounds beyond ADP just to fill a need
  • Proactive Need Awareness — Starts addressing roster gaps early while quality is available
  • Favorites get +5 boost; Busts are skipped entirely
  • Injury Suppression — Non-healthy players excluded from recommendations (configurable)

6. 🤖 Mock Draft Mode

Mock Draft Mode lets you practice your draft strategy before the real thing. When enabled:

  • The AI auto-picks for all other teams, simulating realistic draft behavior with controlled randomness
  • When it's your team's turn, you'll see the "Your Pick" indicator and can make your selection manually
  • Requires "My Team" to be set — the AI needs to know which team you're controlling
  • A "Mock Draft Complete" indicator shows when the draft is finished

Use Mock Draft Mode to test different strategies, see how the AI values fall, and build confidence in your approach before draft day. Save your mock drafts to compare results across strategies in the Draft Trends screen.

7. 🔒 Keeper / Linear Rounds

What Are Keepers?

In keeper leagues, teams retain select players from the previous season. These players are "kept" in specific rounds, meaning those draft slots are pre-filled and not available for regular picks.

Configuring Forced Linear Rounds

Some leagues use keeper rounds that operate in linear order (same pick order as Round 1) regardless of whether the rest of the draft is serpentine. In Settings, you can configure specific rounds as forced linear — the app will adjust the draft flow so those rounds don't follow the serpentine reversal pattern.

8. 📊 Draft Board

The Draft Board provides a full grid view of the entire draft — every pick, every team, every round laid out visually.

  • Color-coded by position — Each position has its own color based on your selected color scheme
  • Tap any cell for player detail including VBD score and value tier
  • See which teams loaded up on certain positions early vs. late
  • Identify league trends and potential trade targets
  • Available during live drafts and from saved drafts

9. 🔍 Player Search

The Player Search screen gives you full access to the player database:

  • Search bar at top — Type any player name to find them instantly
  • Position filter pills — Tap QB, RB, WR, TE, K, or DST to filter by position
  • Sort options — Sort by Rank, ADP, VBD, or Projected Points
  • Hide Drafted toggle — Show only available players

Tap any player to view their full detail card with stats, projected points, VBD, and ESPN links.

10. ⚖️ Trade Analyzer

The Trade Analyzer helps you evaluate potential trades during or after the draft.

How It Works

  • Giving side — Add the players you would trade away (1–3 players)
  • Receiving side — Add the players you would get back (1–3 players)

10-Factor Comparison

The analyzer evaluates trades across 10 factors:

  • VBD Total
  • Avg ADP
  • Avg Rank
  • Positional Value
  • Total TDs
  • Total Yards
  • Health Risk
  • Bye Conflicts
  • VBD Efficiency (per player)
  • Roster Slots

Each player card shows VBD and projected points. Tap the ℹ️ on any comparison category for a plain-English explanation.

Save/Load Trades

Save up to 40 trade scenarios. Load saved trades back into the analyzer or delete via the Saved list.

11. ⭐ Favorites & Busts

Tag players before or during the draft to personalize the AI's behavior:

  • Favorite — Players you're targeting. +5 VBD boost in AI calculations.
  • Bust — Players you want to avoid. Completely excluded from AI recommendations.

Filter Options

  • Type filters: All Players / Favorites / Busts / Both
  • Position filters: Any / QB / RB / WR / TE / K / DST

12. 💾 Saved Drafts & Report Cards

Access your saved drafts from the navigation drawer → Saved Drafts/Report Cards.

Draft Management

Each saved draft shows the save time and has three action buttons:

  • 🟢 Board — Open the full draft board for this draft
  • 🔵 Reports — View the report card with VBD total and rank
  • 🔴 Delete — Remove this draft permanently

Sort drafts by newest or oldest. The app stores up to 30 drafts maximum.

Report Card Tabs

Each report card is organized into tabs:

  • Roster — Full roster view organized by position
  • Bye — Bye week map showing potential conflict weeks
  • Picks — Pick-by-pick breakdown (clickable for player details)
  • Analysis — Round-by-round value bars, cumulative VBD vs league average, best available missed

The report also includes: grade ring, league comparison rank, positional depth bars, and strengths/weaknesses analysis.

Export as JPEG

Tap the blue download FAB to export your report card as a JPEG image to your device gallery (Pictures/DraftExports).

13. 📊 Draft Trends

The Draft Trends screen aggregates data across all your saved drafts to reveal patterns and help you improve over time. Access it from the navigation drawer.

Overview Stats

  • Drafts Analyzed — Total saved drafts being tracked
  • Avg Draft Score — Your average VBD total across all drafts
  • Best/Worst Draft Score — Your high and low watermarks
  • Value Pick Rate — Percentage of picks that were good value
  • Reach Pick Rate — Percentage of picks that were reaches

Draft Position Practiced

Shows how many times you've practiced from each draft slot (e.g., "#1 overall 3×, #6 overall 2×"). Helps ensure you're prepared for any draft position.

Interactive Score Trend Chart

A line chart plotting your VBD scores across drafts over time. Tap any point on the chart to see the exact score for that draft. The chart shows whether you're improving, declining, or steady.

Round 1 Strategy

Breakdown of which position you pick first across all drafts (e.g., "RB first 60%, QB first 20%").

Position Timing

Shows the average round you first draft each position. Tells you at a glance whether you tend to go RB/WR early and QB/TE late.

QB Early vs Late

Splits your drafts into "QB in Rd 1–4" vs "QB in Rd 5+" and compares average scores for each approach. Includes a verdict telling you which strategy historically works better for you.

K/DST Early vs Late

Same analysis for K/DST — compares scores when you draft them before Round 10 vs Round 10+.

Pick Distribution

Per-position percentage table showing how often you draft each position in Early (Rd 1–3), Mid (Rd 4–7), and Late (Rd 8+) tiers.

Most Drafted Players

Players you keep coming back to across multiple drafts (shows players drafted 2+ times).

Trend Direction

Compares your earlier drafts to your recent ones to determine if you're improving, declining, or steady.

14. 📈 Draft Grading Explained

Important: The draft grade is 100% based on total VBD (Value Based Drafting) score.

How It Works

Your grade is determined by the sum of VBD values across all your drafted players. The team with the highest total VBD earns the highest raw score. Draft players who provide the most value above replacement level, and your grade goes up.

Grading Modes

  • Curve Mode — The team with the highest total VBD gets an A+, the lowest gets an F, and everyone else is distributed between. Relative grading.
  • Scale Mode — Uses your raw VBD total directly against a fixed scale. Independent of other teams.

Letter Grade Scale

  • A+ — 90+
  • A — 85–89
  • A- — 80–84
  • B+ — 75–79
  • B — 70–74
  • B- — 65–69
  • C+ — 60–64
  • C — 55–59
  • C- — 50–54
  • F — Below 50

Understanding VBD

In Settings, tap "ℹ️ What is VBD drafting?" for a full explanation of why VBD matters, how replacement level works, and why it beats simple "best player available" drafting. The short version: draft the player who provides the most value ABOVE what you can get for free later.

15. 🏈 Projected Points & Scoring

The app calculates projected fantasy points for every player based on last year's stats and your configured scoring format.

Scoring Formats

  • Standard — No bonus for receptions
  • Half PPR — 0.5 points per reception (default)
  • Full PPR — 1.0 point per reception

How Points Are Calculated

  • QBs — Passing yards × 0.04 (1 pt per 25 yds) + Passing TDs × 4
  • RB/WR/TE — Rush/Rec yards × 0.1 (1 pt per 10 yds) + Rush/Rec TDs × 6 + Receptions × PPR value

Points Breakdown ℹ️

On any player detail card, tap the ℹ️ icon next to "Projected Points" to see a full line-by-line breakdown of how the points are calculated for that player.

Where Points Are Shown

Projected points appear on: Best Available cards, player detail cards, player search results, View Board roster cards, and the Trade Analyzer.

18. 💡 Tips for Draft Day

  1. Refresh data the morning of your draft — Get the latest rankings, injury updates, and ADP shifts
  2. Set "My Team" before the draft starts — The AI can't help you if it doesn't know which team is yours
  3. Use the AI as a guide, not gospel — The recommendations are data-driven and strong, but you know your league
  4. Watch bye weeks — Avoid stacking too many players on the same bye
  5. Tag favorites and busts before the draft — Personalizes the AI and saves decision time
  6. Save your draft when complete — Preserve your draft for report cards and trend analysis
  7. Use the timeline to plan ahead — Check what's projected at your next pick slot
  8. Run mock drafts from different positions — Use Draft Trends to see which strategies work best
  9. Check the Projected Points breakdown — Understand exactly why a player is valued
  10. Trust VBD over name recognition — The best fantasy drafters focus on value above replacement, not star power

19. ❓ FAQ

"Why is my grade low?"

Your draft grade is 100% based on total VBD. In Curve mode, AI-controlled teams also draft efficiently. Even a solid draft might grade as a B if other teams also drafted well. Check your VBD total vs. the league average in the League Comparison section.

"Where are my saved drafts?"

Open the navigation drawer → tap Saved Drafts/Report Cards.

"Where is Draft Trends?"

Open the navigation drawer → tap Draft Trends. It appears below Saved Drafts in the menu. You need at least one saved draft for data to appear.

"Can I undo multiple picks?"

Yes! Tap the Undo button repeatedly to step back through multiple picks.

"What does VBD mean?"

VBD stands for Value Based Drafting. It measures how much value a player provides above the replacement-level player at the same position. Higher VBD = more value above what's freely available. Tap "ℹ️ What is VBD drafting?" in Settings for the full explanation.

"How do I export my report card?"

On the report card screen, tap the blue download FAB. Saved as JPEG to Pictures/DraftExports.

"What is QB Premium?"

QB Premium mode inflates QB values for Superflex or 2QB league formats. Enable in Settings if your league starts 2 QBs or has a Superflex slot.

"What is the Franchise tag?"

The Draft Advisor labels the top 3 players at QB, RB, WR, and TE with a 🏷️ Franchise tag. This highlights elite positional talent that commands premium draft capital.

"How does PPR affect recommendations?"

In Half-PPR or Full PPR modes, the AI gives bonus weight to players with high reception counts from last season. High-target WRs and pass-catching RBs rise in recommendations. Set your scoring format in Settings.

"What are FLEX slots?"

FLEX slots accept RB, WR, or TE. When configured, the AI ensures you have enough flex-eligible depth to fill all required positions plus FLEX slots. Pass-catching RBs get extra value for their multi-position utility.

"Why does the timeline show my pick in green?"

Green-highlighted slots are your projected draft picks based on the serpentine order and your team position. Plan ahead based on what might be available at your next slot.

"How does the interactive chart work?"

On the Draft Trends screen, tap or drag along the Score Trend chart. The nearest data point highlights with a white dot and shows the exact score in a tooltip above it.
