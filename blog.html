New Feature — QB Flex Setting

Hi All,

Really excited about this one. The new QB Flex setting puts a premium on the QB position in your league — and it completely changes the draft dynamic in the best way possible.

If your league runs a superflex or 2-QB format, this setting tells the AI that quarterbacks carry extra weight. The scarcity math shifts, the value calculations adjust, and suddenly you're seeing QBs recommended earlier and more aggressively. It's a game-changer for leagues where elite QBs are gold.

Whether you're running a mock to test strategies or going live on draft day, QB Flex makes the experience feel tailored to how your league actually scores. Mock with it a few times and watch how different your roster construction looks compared to a standard build. It's honestly one of my favorite additions — turns the whole draft on its head in a fun way.

Give it a shot and let me know what you think. Happy drafting,

— Jeff

5 Ways the AI Advisor Gives You an Edge

The AI recommendation engine isn't just another rankings list. It evaluates every available player in real time based on your specific draft situation. Here's how to use it to its full potential:

  • Trust the scarcity alerts. When the AI flags a position running dry, it's usually right. Don't wait another round.
  • Watch for value steals. If a player's ADP says round 4 but they're still on the board in round 6, the AI will flag it.
  • Use Favorites strategically. Mark 10-15 players you've researched as favorites — the +3 bonus helps the AI align with your pre-draft prep.
  • Don't ignore bye week warnings. Stacking 3 starters on the same bye is a recipe for a week 7 disaster.
  • Read the reasoning bullets. The AI tells you why. If you disagree with the logic, override it — you're the GM.
