Core Feature

🤖 AI-Powered Draft Recommendations

Go beyond simple rankings. The AI engine evaluates every available player through five key lenses — factoring in player health status — and tells you exactly why it likes a pick so you can draft with confidence.

💰

Value

Has this player fallen past their ADP? The engine spots draft-day bargains so you never miss a steal sitting right in front of you.

⚡

Scarcity

How quickly is a position drying up? When startable talent is disappearing, the engine raises the urgency before it's too late.

🎯

Team Need

What does your roster still need? The engine checks your lineup against your position requirements and prioritizes the gaps.

📅

Bye Week

Nobody wants half their starters idle the same week. The engine penalizes players whose bye week overlaps with your existing roster.

🏆

Elite Talent

Some players are generational starters. The engine gives a bonus to top-tier positional talent so you don't pass on a league-winner.

🏥

Health Status

Player health matters. The engine applies a scoring penalty for Questionable and Out players, and surfaces injury warnings so you draft with eyes wide open.

🧠 Transparent Reasoning

Every recommendation comes with clear reasoning bullets explaining which factors drove the pick. No black box — you see exactly why the AI likes a player.

🏥 Injury-Aware Scoring

Non-healthy players (Questionable, Out) receive a -15 point penalty. Injury warnings are displayed directly in the advisor text so you never draft blind.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it work offline?
Yes — 100%. All player data is bundled in the app. The AI advisor, pick grading, and draft tracking all run on your device with no internet connection required. Perfect for draft parties with spotty Wi-Fi.
How many teams and rounds does it support?
Up to 30 rounds and as many teams as your league needs. Team names, draft order, and position requirements are all fully configurable before the draft starts.
Can I use it for keeper or dynasty leagues?
Yes. You can configure 1–10 linear rounds at the start of the draft for keeper picks, then the app switches to serpentine for the remaining rounds. Great for leagues where the first few rounds are pre-determined.
What is Mock Draft Mode?
Mock Draft Mode lets AI auto-pick for all non-user teams, simulating a realistic draft with controlled randomness. It's perfect for practice runs before your real draft day. Enable it with a single checkbox in draft settings — an orange "MOCK" badge appears in the header so you always know you're in practice mode.
How does the AI advisor decide who to recommend?
The engine scores every available player across five weighted factors: ADP value (30%), positional scarcity (25%), your team's roster needs (25%), bye week conflicts (10%), and elite talent bonus (10%). Injury and bust penalties are applied on top. Every recommendation includes reasoning bullets so you can agree or override it.
How does the draft grading system work?
After the draft, every team receives a letter grade (A+ through F) based on a curve-normalized scoring system. Grades factor in value picks, reaches, average ADP difference, roster balance, and health risk from injured players (-2 penalty per injured player). Each team also gets a draft strategy classification like RB Heavy, Zero RB, or Balanced.
What are Favorites and Busts?
You can mark any player as a ⭐ Favorite or 💣 Bust — they're mutually exclusive. Favorites are highlighted yellow and get a +3 AI bonus. Busts are highlighted red and get a -15 penalty. These persist through app restarts and data refreshes, and they're visible across all views with emoji indicators.
Where does the player data come from?
Player rankings, ADP, and stats come from publicly available ESPN data, bundled directly in the app. Data can be refreshed from a remote source, with auto-refresh on first run.
Does it support both serpentine and linear drafts?
Yes. Choose serpentine (snake) or linear (straight) draft order during setup. You can also combine them — linear for keeper rounds, then serpentine for the rest.
Is it really free? What's the catch?
It's free, ad-free, and requires no account or sign-up. No in-app purchases, no paywalls, no data collection. The app was built by a fantasy football fan for fantasy football fans.
