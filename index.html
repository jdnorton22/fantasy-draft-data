WHAT IT DOES

AI draft advisor

Scores every player on value, scarcity, team need, bye weeks and injuries, and tells you why.

Tiers and pick grades

See when a tier is about to run out. Every pick is graded from Elite to Big Reach.

Mock drafts

Practice with AI-controlled teams that draft like real league mates.

Traded picks New

Swap picks 1-for-1, 1-for-many or many-for-many, before or during the draft. Traded picks show on the board.

Keeper leagues

Set keeper rounds, then draft serpentine or linear.

Draft board and clock

Live board, roster needs, recent picks and a color-coded pick timer.

Report cards

A-to-F grades for every team after the draft. Save and share your results.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. Set upName your league, teams, rounds and roster rules.
  2. DraftFollow the Best Available card, or search any player.
  3. ReviewCheck the grades, history and report cards.
