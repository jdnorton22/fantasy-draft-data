Frequently Asked Questions

Does it work offline?
Yes — 100%. All player data is bundled in the app. The AI advisor, pick grading, and draft tracking all run on your device with no internet connection required. Perfect for draft parties with spotty Wi-Fi.
How does the AI advisor decide who to recommend?
The engine scores every available player across five weighted factors: ADP value (30%), positional scarcity (25%), your team's roster needs (25%), bye week conflicts (10%), and elite talent bonus (10%). Injury and bust penalties are applied on top. Every recommendation includes reasoning bullets.
Can I use it for keeper or dynasty leagues?
Yes. Configure 1–10 linear rounds at the start for keeper picks, then the app switches to serpentine for the remaining rounds. Supports both serpentine and linear draft flows.
Is it really free? What's the catch?
It's free, ad-free, and requires no account or sign-up. No in-app purchases, no paywalls, no data collection. Built by a fantasy football fan for fantasy football fans.
